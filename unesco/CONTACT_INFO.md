## Submission Process

### Recommended Approach

1. **Email Submission** to:
   - ge.vernizzi@unesco.org (Executive Assistant)
   - a.melo@unesco.org (Director)

2. **Subject Line**:
   "Proposal for WIA Braille - Universal Braille System for 7,000+ Languages"

3. **Attachments**:
   - COVER_LETTER.md (PDF)
   - EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md (PDF)
   - PROPOSAL.md (PDF)
   - Link to full documentation (GitHub)

4. **Follow-up**:
   - Allow 2-4 weeks for initial review
   - Be prepared for presentation request
   - Provide additional materials as needed

### Timing Considerations

**Best Times for Submission**:
- **Before World Braille Day** (January 4) - High relevance
- **March-April** - Pre-General Conference planning
- **September-October** - General Conference period

**Avoid**:
- Late December - Early January (holidays)
- August (summer break)
