## Usage Guidelines

### Image Specifications by Platform:

**Website Hero**: 1920x1080px (16:9), JPEG/PNG, max 500KB
**Press Release**: 1200x900px (4:3), JPEG, max 300KB
**Instagram Post**: 1080x1080px (1:1), JPEG, max 1MB
**Instagram Story**: 1080x1920px (9:16), JPEG/PNG, max 1MB
**Twitter/X**: 1200x675px (16:9), JPEG/PNG, max 5MB
**LinkedIn**: 1200x627px (1.91:1), JPEG/PNG, max 5MB
**Facebook**: 1200x630px (1.91:1), JPEG, max 8MB

### Alt Text Templates:

For accessibility, always include alt text:

1. **Hero Image**: "Glowing 8-dot braille cell connected to 7,000 points of light representing languages around a translucent Earth globe"

2. **Press Image**: "Hands reading 8-dot braille with holographic projections of multiple writing systems transforming into IPA symbols"

3. **Social Square**: "Elegant 3D gold braille cell floating against blue-teal gradient with ring of abstract language symbols"

4. **UNESCO Image**: "Formal document with embossed braille on conference table with world map showing 31 African countries"

5. **Hangul Bridge**: "Split-screen showing 1443 Hangul creation connecting through golden thread to 2025 WIA Braille innovation"

### Color Psychology:

- **Blue** (#003d7a, #1976d2): Trust, stability, professionalism, global standards
- **Gold** (#ffd700, #c9a961): Innovation, value, illumination, Korean heritage
- **Teal** (#3e92cc): Accessibility, technology, modern, friendly
- **White**: Clarity, simplicity, openness, universal

### Tone Consistency:

All images should convey:
- ✅ Hope (not pity)
- ✅ Empowerment (not charity)
- ✅ Innovation (not complexity)
- ✅ Universality (not exclusivity)
- ✅ Professionalism (not amateurism)

## DALL-E Generation Tips

1. **Be Specific**: The more specific your prompt, the better the result
2. **Iterate**: Generate 4 variations, then refine the best one
3. **Test Sizes**: Generate at highest resolution, then resize for different platforms
4. **Consistency**: Use same color codes across all images for brand consistency
5. **Accessibility**: Always ensure high contrast for readability
6. **Cultural Sensitivity**: Review images for unintentional cultural stereotypes
7. **Rights**: All DALL-E generated images can be used commercially per OpenAI terms
