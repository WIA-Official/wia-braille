Chapter 8

Future of eVTOL

Market Outlook and Technology Roadmap

🚀 eVTOL Market Outlook

eVTOL will be one of the 21st centurys most revolutionary transport innovations. Multiple market research agencies predict explosive growth of eVTOL and UAM markets.

Major Market Research Results

💰 Global eVTOL/UAM Market Size Projections

  • Morgan Stanley (2020): $1.5T by 2040 (Air taxi $850B, Cargo $650B)
  • Roland Berger (2023): $90B by 2050 (2.3B annual passengers)
  • McKinsey (2022): $500B by 2035 (cumulative market size)
  • ARK Invest (2023): $800B by 2030 (including autonomous flight)

For these projections to realize, several prerequisites needed: Continued battery technology advances, large-scale certification completion, vertiport infrastructure construction, regulatory framework establishment, public acceptance.

📅 Technology Roadmap (2025-2050)

2025-2027: Initial Commercialization

  • Joby, Archer, Volocopter, etc. first commercial services start
  • Airport shuttle routes focus (city-airport)
  • Pilot onboard, limited autonomous functions
  • Price: $5-8 per mile (premium service)
  • Battery: 250-300 Wh/kg, range 50-250km
  • 50-100 vertiports operating worldwide
  • Annual passengers: 1 million

2028-2030: Growth and Expansion

  • Add short-range urban routes
  • Partial autonomous flight (Level 3) introduction
  • Price: $3-5 per mile (middle class accessible)
  • Battery: 350-400 Wh/kg (early solid-state)
  • Range: 300-400km
  • Vertiports: 500-1,000 (major cities)
  • Annual passengers: 150 million
  • Cargo eVTOL full operations (medical, urgent delivery)

2031-2035: Popularization

  • Fully autonomous flight (Level 4-5) commercialization
  • Price: $2-4 per mile (public transport competitive)
  • Battery: 450-500 Wh/kg (solid-state mainstream)
  • Range: 500-700km
  • Vertiports: 5,000-10,000 (worldwide)
  • Annual passengers: 1.5 billion
  • Scheduled route operations (timetable-based like subway)
  • Fully integrated MaaS (seamless connection with ground transport)

2036-2040: Maturity Stage

  • Partial replacement of domestic flights (within 500km)
  • Price: $1-2 per mile (bus/subway level)
  • Hydrogen fuel cell eVTOL introduction (1,000km+ range)
  • Battery: 600+ Wh/kg (lithium-sulfur, lithium-air)
  • Vertiports: 50,000 (including mid-size cities)
  • Annual passengers: 2.3 billion
  • Total transport modal share: 5-10%
  • Carbon neutrality achieved (renewable energy charging)

2041-2050: Continued Innovation

  • Personal eVTOL ownership spreads (like cars)
  • Ultra-high-speed eVTOL (500+ km/h, tilt-wing or compound designs)
  • Complete noise elimination technology (below 50 dB)
  • Brain-computer interface control
  • Urban 3D traffic network completion (altitude-based layers)
  • Integration with space tourism (ground-space integrated transport)

🔬 Next-Generation Technologies

1. Battery Technology Innovation

eVTOL future depends on battery technology. From current 250-300 Wh/kg, targeting 500 Wh/kg by 2030s, 1,000 Wh/kg by 2040s.

  • Solid-State Batteries (2028~): 400-500 Wh/kg, 1/5 charging time, zero fire risk
  • Lithium-Sulfur Batteries (2032~): 600-800 Wh/kg, low cost
  • Lithium-Air Batteries (2040~): 1,000+ Wh/kg, theoretical limit
  • Graphene Batteries (research): Ultra-fast charging, long life

2. Hydrogen Fuel Cells

For long-range operations (500km+), hydrogen fuel cell eVTOL will be developed. Hydrogen has much higher energy density per weight than batteries - suitable for long distances.

  • H2FLY (Germany): Developing hydrogen fuel cell aircraft
  • ZeroAvia (UK/US): Hydrogen electric propulsion systems
  • Hyundai: Hydrogen eVTOL research (utilizing automotive hydrogen technology)

3. Fully Autonomous Flight

By 2030s, pilotless fully autonomous flight will become mainstream. AI handles all flight phases, ground control monitors dozens to hundreds simultaneously.

  • Computer vision for obstacle recognition
  • Deep learning for real-time optimal path calculation
  • 5G/6G networks for ultra-low latency communication
  • Digital twin for simulation-based safety verification

4. 3D Altitude Traffic Network

Divide urban airspace into multiple altitudes for efficient utilization:

  • 300-500m: Low-speed multicopters (tourism, short-range)
  • 500-1,000m: Medium-speed air taxis (urban travel)
  • 1,000-1,500m: High-speed tilt-rotors (long-range)
  • 1,500-3,000m: Cargo and special missions

🌏 Social Impact

1. Urban Planning Changes

eVTOL will change cities physical structure. Vertiports installed on all major building rooftops, parking lots converted to charging stations. Cities expand vertically not horizontally, movement between high-rise buildings becomes very fast.

2. Economic Impact

New jobs created: eVTOL manufacturing, vertiport operations, maintenance, air traffic control, software development, battery manufacturing, infrastructure construction. McKinsey predicts over 1 million jobs globally by 2040.

3. Environmental Benefits

Ground traffic reduction improves urban air quality. Charging with renewable energy significantly reduces carbon emissions. Also, no road expansion needed preserves green spaces.

4. Equity and Accessibility

Initially premium service but by 2030s price will drop to public transport level. Island regions, mountainous areas, developing nations with inadequate infrastructure will greatly benefit.

⚠️ Challenges

1. Technical Challenges

  • Battery energy density improvement (still 1/40 of jet fuel)
  • Large-scale charging infrastructure construction
  • Overcoming weather constraints (icing, strong winds, lightning)
  • Cybersecurity (hack prevention)

2. Regulatory and Policy

  • International standardization (mutual recognition between countries)
  • Airspace management (coexistence with existing aircraft)
  • Noise regulations (resident acceptance)
  • Privacy protection (flight data, passenger information)

3. Social Acceptance

  • Public safety perception (major impact from initial accidents)
  • Privacy concerns (looking down from sky)
  • Equity issues (concern about wealthy-only service)

✨ The 2050 eVTOL World

In 2050, you summon an eVTOL with your smartphone in the morning. 5 minutes later, with quiet humming, an eVTOL lands on your apartment rooftop vertiport. When you board, the door closes and automatically takes off. There is no pilot. AI controls everything.

Outside the window you see hundreds of eVTOL flying orderly at their respective altitudes. Like a 3D highway. 20 minutes later you arrive at your office building rooftop 60km away. A distance that would take 2 hours on the ground, in 20 minutes without traffic.

In the afternoon you travel with a friend to a coastal city. 250km in 1 hour, have lunch, enjoy the afternoon, and return home for dinner. The cost is slightly more than high-speed rail. eVTOL has become routine transportation like buses or subways.

The sky is no longer just for airplanes. Its everyones travel space. Traffic congestion is a thing of the past, cities are cleaner and quieter, and people travel farther, more frequently, and more easily.

This is the future eVTOL promises.

🎯 Conclusion

eVTOL is not just a new vehicle. Its an innovation that will change urban transport paradigm, redefine city planning, and greatly improve peoples quality of life. When first commercial services start in 2025, it will grow explosively over the next 25 years to become mainstream transport used by billions annually by 2050. Many challenges remain - battery technology, autonomous flight, infrastructure construction - but industry and government are actively solving them one by one.

Humanity is once again making sky dreams reality 120 years after the Wright brothers. This time, its sky for everyone. The age of eVTOL is coming.
