Chapter 7

eVTOL Manufacturers

Leading Companies and Their Aircraft

🏭 eVTOL Industry Ecosystem

As of 2024, over 700 eVTOL projects worldwide. About 50 manufacturers have built actual flyable prototypes, and a dozen companies are seriously pursuing certification. This chapter examines major eVTOL manufacturers leading the industry.

🇺🇸 US Manufacturers

1. Joby Aviation

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2009 (Santa Cruz, California)
Founder: JoeBen Bevirt
IPO: 2021 NYSE (Ticker: JOBY), Market cap ~$5B
Total Funding: Over $2B
Major Investors: Toyota, Uber, Intel, Baillie Gifford

Joby S4 Specifications

  • Design Type: Tilt-rotor (6 propellers)
  • Passengers: 4 + 1 pilot
  • Top Speed: 320 km/h
  • Range: 240 km
  • Maximum Altitude: 4,600m
  • Noise: 65 dB @ 100m (takeoff/landing), 45 dB @ 100m (cruise)

Joby currently leads the eVTOL industry. Passed FAA Stage 4 of 5 certification, targeting 2025 commercial operations. Acquired Uber Elevate in 2020 to secure air taxi platform. Closely collaborating with Toyota to prepare mass production, planning to start production at California Marina factory from 2024.

Jobys strength is outstanding performance. 320 km/h speed and 240km range are top among current eVTOL in development. Very low noise suitable for urban operations. Especially 45 dB cruise noise is quiet enough to blend with ambient noise.

2. Archer Aviation

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2018 (Palo Alto, California)
Founders: Brett Adcock, Adam Goldstein
IPO: 2021 NYSE (Ticker: ACHR)
Total Funding: $1.5B
Major Investors: United Airlines, Stellantis

Archer Midnight Specifications

  • Design Type: Lift+Cruise (12 lift rotors + 6 tilting propellers)
  • Passengers: 4 + 1 pilot
  • Top Speed: 240 km/h
  • Range: 160 km
  • Charging Time: 10 minutes (optimized for continuous operations)

Archer especially emphasizes fast turnaround time. Can fly again after 10-minute charge for multiple daily flights. Signed $1 billion contract with United Airlines, planning Newark Airport to Manhattan air taxi route.

3. Wisk Aero

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2019 (Boeing + Kitty Hawk joint venture)
Headquarters: Mountain View, California
Owner: Boeing 100% (acquired Kitty Hawk 2022)
Investment: Full Boeing support

Wisk 6th Generation Specifications

  • Design Type: Lift+Cruise (12 lift rotors + 4 pusher propellers)
  • Passengers: 4 (no pilot - fully autonomous)
  • Top Speed: 220 km/h
  • Range: 140 km
  • Feature: Worlds first unmanned air taxi target

Wisks biggest differentiator is fully autonomous flight. Operating without pilots reduces operating costs and increases safety (eliminates human error). Leveraging Boeings aviation expertise and capital, working closely with FAA to pioneer autonomous eVTOL certification paths.

🇩🇪 German Manufacturers

4. Volocopter

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2011 (Bruchsal, Germany)
Founders: Stephan Wolf, Alexander Zosel
Total Funding: $500M
Major Investors: Daimler, Intel, Geely

Volocopter VoloCity Specifications

  • Design Type: Multicopter (18 fixed rotors)
  • Passengers: 1 + 1 pilot
  • Top Speed: 110 km/h
  • Range: 35 km
  • Feature: Worlds first manned electric multicopter (2013)

Volocopter is eVTOL pioneer. Achieved worlds first manned electric multicopter flight in 2011, planning demonstration operations at 2024 Paris Olympics. EASA SC-VTOL certification in progress - likely first commercial in Europe. Specialized for short distances - optimal for 5-15km urban trips. 18 rotors high redundancy for very safe, low noise suitable for city operations.

5. Lilium

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2015 (Munich, Germany)
Founders: Daniel Wiegand, Sebastian Born, Patrick Nathen, Matthias Meiner
IPO: 2021 NASDAQ (Ticker: LILM)
Total Funding: $1B

Lilium Jet Specifications

  • Design Type: Ducted fan tilting (36 electric jet engines)
  • Passengers: 6 + 1 pilot
  • Top Speed: 280 km/h
  • Range: 250 km
  • Feature: Unique ducted fan technology

Lilium uses unique electric ducted fan technology. 36 small jet engines embedded in wings tilt to generate vertical lift and forward thrust. High speed and long range suitable for intercity travel. Building large-scale production facilities in Florida and Germany.

🇨🇳 Chinese Manufacturers

6. EHang

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2014 (Guangzhou, China)
Founder: Huazhi Hu
IPO: 2019 NASDAQ (Ticker: EH)
Type Certificate: 2021 CAAC (worlds first)

EHang EH216 Specifications

  • Design Type: Multicopter (16 rotors)
  • Passengers: 2 (no pilot - fully autonomous)
  • Top Speed: 130 km/h
  • Range: 30 km
  • Feature: Worlds first Type Certified eVTOL

EHang obtained worlds first eVTOL Type Certificate from CAAC in 2021. Designed for fully autonomous operation without pilots, already demonstrating commercial operations in China, Dubai, and Spain. Rapidly commercializing with strong Chinese government support.

🇰🇷 Korean Manufacturers

7. Hyundai Supernal

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2020 (Hyundai Motor Group)
Headquarters: Washington DC, USA
Investment: Full Hyundai Motor Group support
Target: 2028 commercialization

Supernal S-A2 Specifications (Expected)

  • Design Type: Tilt-rotor
  • Passengers: 4-5
  • Top Speed: 240+ km/h
  • Range: 100+ km
  • Feature: Leveraging Hyundais mass production expertise

Hyundai Supernal leads Korean eVTOL development. Utilizing Hyundai Motor Groups automotive manufacturing experience and electric vehicle technology to target stable, affordable eVTOL. FAA certification in progress, closely collaborating with Korean governments K-UAM initiative. Hyundai produces millions of cars annually - mass production capability expected to play important role in popularizing eVTOL. Plan to rapidly increase production after 2028 commercialization to reduce prices.

🇬🇧 UK Manufacturers

8. Vertical Aerospace

🚁 Company Overview

Founded: 2016 (Bristol, UK)
Founder: Stephen Fitzpatrick
IPO: 2021 NYSE (Ticker: EVTL)
Major Customers: American Airlines, Virgin Atlantic, Avolon

Vertical VX4 Specifications

  • Design Type: Lift+Cruise (8 lift rotors + 4 tilting propellers)
  • Passengers: 4 + 1 pilot
  • Top Speed: 320 km/h
  • Range: 160 km
  • Feature: Europes largest eVTOL pre-orders

Vertical Aerospace secured over 1,400 pre-orders including American Airlines (250), Virgin Atlantic (150), Avolon (500). Closely collaborating with UK government to make London an eVTOL hub.

📊 Manufacturer Comparison

🏆 Each Manufacturers Strengths

  • Joby: Best performance (speed, range), certification progress #1
  • Archer: Fast turnaround, major airline partnerships
  • Wisk: Autonomous flight leader, Boeing support
  • Volocopter: Longest experience, European market leader
  • Lilium: Unique technology, long-range operations
  • EHang: Worlds first certification, rapid commercialization
  • Supernal: Mass production capability, automotive technology utilization
  • Vertical: Large-scale pre-orders, airline partnerships

🌐 Global Competition Landscape

eVTOL industry currently US-led. Most major players are American companies like Joby, Archer, Wisk, Beta Technologies, with over 70% of total investment concentrated in US companies. Europe has strong technical capabilities but relatively less capital. Volocopter and Lilium lead with EASAs systematic certification framework (SC-VTOL) as strength. China rapidly catching up government-led. EHang obtained worlds first certification, followers like AutoFlight, XPeng HT also growing fast. Chinese market scale and government support are strengths. Korea pursuing technology independence through Hyundai and Hanwhas eVTOL development, building infrastructure and systems together with K-UAM initiative. Planning technology validation through 2025 K-UAM Grand Challenge.
