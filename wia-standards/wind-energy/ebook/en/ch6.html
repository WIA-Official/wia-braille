6.3 Grid Code Compliance

Grid codes specify technical requirements for connecting and operating generation facilities. Non-compliance can result in disconnection and financial penalties.

6.3.1 Common Grid Code Requirements

  • Fault Ride-Through: Remain connected during voltage sags (typically 15% voltage for 625ms)
  • Frequency Response: Adjust active power in response to grid frequency deviations
  • Voltage Control: Maintain voltage within specified range through reactive power control
  • Power Quality: Limit harmonics, flicker, and other disturbances
  • Ramp Rate Control: Limit rate of power output change (typically 10-30%/minute)
  • Dispatch Instructions: Follow grid operator commands for power setpoints
  • Communication: Provide real-time data to grid operator (SCADA integration)

Comprehensive safety management systems ensure regulatory compliance while protecting workers and the public.

6.7 Audit and Certification

Regular audits and certifications verify compliance with standards and identify improvement opportunities.

6.7.1 Audit Types and Frequencies

  • Internal Audits: Quarterly reviews of critical processes and controls
  • Management System Audits: Annual ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 audits
  • Regulatory Audits: As required by authorities (typically annual or biennial)
  • Financial Audits: Annual independent audit of financial statements
  • Cybersecurity Audits: Annual penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
  • Technical Audits: Periodic review of turbine condition and performance

6.10 Emerging Compliance Trends

The regulatory landscape for wind energy continues to evolve, with new requirements emerging in areas such as climate risk disclosure, circular economy, and social impact.

  • Climate Risk Disclosure: Requirements to report on climate-related financial risks (TCFD framework)
  • Blade Recycling: Emerging regulations on end-of-life management and circular economy
  • Social Impact Reporting: ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) reporting expectations
  • Grid Services: Enhanced requirements for grid support capabilities as renewable penetration increases
  • Cybersecurity Standards: Stricter requirements following increased threat awareness
