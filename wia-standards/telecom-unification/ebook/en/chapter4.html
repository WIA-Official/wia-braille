Chapter 4

Communication Services

Voice, video, messaging, and real-time translation services

📞 Voice Services

High-quality voice calling is the foundation of inter-Korean communication. WIA-UNI-006 implements VoLTE (Voice over LTE) and VoNR (Voice over NR) for crystal-clear calls with minimal latency.

Voice Quality

  • Codec: EVS (Enhanced Voice Services) - HD voice quality
  • Bitrate: Adaptive 5.9-128 kbps
  • Latency: <50ms end-to-end
  • Packet Loss: <0.5% with error correction

Real-Time Dialect Translation

After 70+ years of separation, North and South Korean dialects have diverged. Real-time translation bridges this gap:

  • Neural machine translation optimized for Korean dialects
  • Sub-second translation latency
  • Context-aware: understands formal vs. casual speech
  • Preserves emotional tone and intent
  • Optional: translation can be disabled for users who prefer authentic dialect

Translation Example

North Korean speaker: "동무, 이거 잘 됐네요" (Comrade, this worked out well)

Translated to South: "친구, 이거 잘 됐네요" (Friend, this worked out well)

The system automatically adjusts vocabulary while preserving meaning and emotional tone.

🎥 Video Services

Video calling enables face-to-face connection across the DMZ, crucial for separated families who haven't seen each other in decades.

Video Quality Options

  • SD (480p): 500 kbps - works on 3G/4G
  • HD (720p): 1.5 Mbps - recommended for 4G/5G
  • Full HD (1080p): 3 Mbps - 5G, family calls
  • 4K (2160p): 8 Mbps - special occasions, VR-ready

Advanced Features

  • Multi-party Calls: Up to 50 participants
  • Screen Sharing: Share photos, documents
  • Virtual Backgrounds: Privacy in shared spaces
  • Recording: Family calls can be recorded (with consent)
  • Live Translation Subtitles: Real-time text translation overlay

💬 Messaging Services

SMS/MMS

  • Traditional SMS works across DMZ
  • MMS supports photos, videos, voice messages
  • No length limits for family messages
  • Automatic translation option

RCS (Rich Communication Services)

  • Modern messaging with read receipts
  • Group chats with up to 100 members
  • File sharing (up to 100MB per file)
  • Location sharing
  • End-to-end encryption for privacy

🚨 Emergency Services

Unified emergency services save lives by enabling cross-border coordination.

112/119 Integration

  • Dial 112 (police) or 119 (medical/fire) from anywhere in Korea
  • Automatic location detection (GPS + cell tower triangulation)
  • Cross-border emergency response coordination
  • Medical records access with patient consent
  • Real-time translation for emergency operators

Emergency Scenarios

  • DMZ Accidents: Coordinated response from both sides
  • Cross-Border Medical: Patient transport across DMZ
  • Natural Disasters: Joint disaster response
  • Missing Persons: Coordinated search operations

🎯 Quality of Service (QoS)

Service Priority Levels

  • Priority 1: Emergency services (112/119)
  • Priority 2: Family humanitarian calls
  • Priority 3: Business voice calls
  • Priority 4: Standard data services
  • Priority 5: Background data (downloads, updates)

Performance Guarantees

  • Call Setup Time: <2 seconds
  • Call Drop Rate: <0.1%
  • Voice Quality (MOS): 4.0+ (out of 5.0)
  • Video Buffering: <1% of call duration
