Roaming & Billing

Economics of unified telecommunications and zero-cost inter-Korean communication

🌏 Inter-Korean Roaming

The revolutionary aspect of WIA-UNI-006 is the concept of "One Korea Roaming" - treating inter-Korean communication as domestic traffic rather than international roaming. A person crossing the DMZ should experience the same seamless connectivity as taking the subway from Seoul to Incheon.

Roaming Principles

  • Zero Roaming Charges: No fees for cross-border calls, messages, or data
  • Same SIM Card: Works in both North and South without changes
  • Seamless Handover: Network switches automatically at DMZ
  • Unified Billing: Single monthly bill regardless of usage location

Technical Implementation

The roaming layer uses standard 3GPP roaming protocols with custom inter-Korean extensions:

  • S8 Interface: Direct connection between North and South core networks
  • Diameter Protocol: For authentication and authorization
  • GTP Tunneling: Secure data transport across DMZ
  • ENUM/DNS: Number portability between regions

💰 Billing Model

Free Humanitarian Services

All family-related communications between North and South are completely free:

  • Voice calls (unlimited duration)
  • Video calls (HD quality, unlimited)
  • SMS/MMS messages
  • Family photo/video sharing

Funded Services

Business and commercial traffic generates revenue to subsidize humanitarian services:

Service Type Pricing Revenue Use
Business Calls ₩100/min 70% infrastructure, 30% humanitarian fund
Premium Data (Business) ₩30,000/100GB 50% infrastructure, 50% expansion
International Roaming Standard rates 100% revenue to Unified Korea Telecom
Content Services Varies Revenue sharing with content providers

Unified Korea Telecom (UKT) Billing

UKT operates a unified billing platform processing charges from both regions:

  • Single billing system for all 51 million users
  • Transparent itemization (domestic vs. cross-border)
  • Multiple payment options (bank transfer, mobile payment, cryptocurrency)
  • Automatic currency conversion (KRW ↔ local currency)

📊 Economic Model

Revenue Streams

  • Business Communications: $420M/year
  • Premium Data Plans: $680M/year
  • International Roaming: $240M/year
  • Content Services: $180M/year
  • Government Subsidies: $480M/year
  • Total: $2B/year

Operational Costs

  • Infrastructure Maintenance: $800M/year
  • Network Operations: $400M/year
  • Customer Support: $150M/year
  • Security & Privacy: $100M/year
  • Humanitarian Fund: $420M/year
  • Total: $1.87B/year

Financial Sustainability

The model generates $130M/year surplus, which funds network expansion, 6G research, and increased humanitarian services. By year 5, the network becomes fully self-sustaining without government subsidies.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Verification

To prevent abuse of free family services while protecting privacy:

Registration Process

  • One-time family relationship verification
  • Submitted through Red Cross or government agencies
  • Privacy-preserving: only relationship verified, not content
  • Approved within 48 hours

Eligible Relationships

  • Parents, children, grandparents, grandchildren
  • Siblings, spouses
  • Extended family (uncles, aunts, cousins) - limited quota
  • Special cases reviewed by humanitarian committee

💳 Payment Methods

South Korean Users

  • Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay)
  • Mobile payments (Samsung Pay, Naver Pay, Kakao Pay)
  • Bank transfers
  • Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum) - for privacy

North Korean Users

  • Local bank transfers
  • Mobile payment systems
  • Cash payments at telecom offices
  • International payment cards (where available)
