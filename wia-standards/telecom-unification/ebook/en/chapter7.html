🔧 Technical Requirements

Infrastructure Components

  • 5G Base Stations: 45,000 total (15,000 new, 30,000 upgraded)
  • Fiber Optic Cable: 120,000 km inter-Korean fiber
  • Core Network: 12 regional data centers
  • Internet Gateway: 10 Tbps capacity
  • Power Infrastructure: Redundant power for 99.99% uptime

Equipment Specifications

  • 5G RAN: Samsung, Ericsson, Huawei equipment
  • Core Network: Nokia, Cisco, Juniper
  • CDN: Akamai, Cloudflare, local providers
  • Security: Palo Alto, F5, specialized Korean solutions

👷 Deployment Strategy

DMZ First Approach

Start with 100% coverage of the DMZ to enable seamless handover:

  • Base station every 500m along DMZ (248 km)
  • Redundant fiber optic connections
  • Joint operations center in Panmunjom
  • Special security protocols for sensitive border area

City-Centric Expansion

  • Tier 1 Cities: Seoul, Pyongyang, Busan - 2026
  • Tier 2 Cities: Incheon, Kaesong, Wonsan - 2027
  • Tier 3 Cities: Daegu, Daejeon, Hamhung - 2028
  • Rural Areas: Progressive rollout 2028-2030

🧪 Testing & Quality Assurance

Pre-Launch Testing (2025-2026)

  • Lab Testing: 6 months of equipment integration testing
  • Field Trials: 100 test users in controlled environment
  • Security Audit: Independent penetration testing
  • Load Testing: Simulate 1M concurrent users

Pilot Program (2026-2027)

  • 1,000 separated families selected for pilot
  • 3 months of supervised testing
  • Daily feedback collection and rapid iteration
  • Success criteria: 95% satisfaction, <0.1% call failure rate

💼 Organizational Structure

Unified Korea Telecom (UKT)

  • CEO: Appointed jointly by both governments (5-year term)
  • CTO: International telecom expert
  • COO: Shared role (1 from North, 1 from South)
  • CISO: Security specialist with international credentials
  • Staff: 5,000 employees (50/50 split North/South)

Advisory Board

  • Representatives from both Korean governments
  • International telecom experts (ITU, 3GPP)
  • Humanitarian organizations (Red Cross, UN)
  • Academic institutions
  • User representatives (family associations)

Success Story: First Call (2026)

On July 27, 2026 - the 73rd anniversary of the armistice - Mrs. Kim in Seoul made the first official call to her brother in Pyongyang. They hadn't spoken in 68 years. The call lasted 2 hours and 14 minutes, with perfect HD quality and real-time translation. This moment proved that technology can heal the wounds of division.
