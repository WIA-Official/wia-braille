Dimming Method Comparison

  • PWM: On/off switching at high frequency. Best color accuracy but may flicker.
  • DC: Voltage reduction. Flicker-free but may shift colors at low brightness.
  • Hybrid: PWM at high brightness, DC at low brightness. Best of both worlds.
  • FALD: Zone-based dimming for LCD. Improves contrast and reduces blooming.

Burn-in Prevention Techniques

  • Pixel Shift: Subtly move image to prevent static element burn-in
  • Logo Detection: Detect and dim static logos automatically
  • ABL (Automatic Brightness Limiter): Reduce brightness on high APL content
  • Subpixel Compensation: Adjust voltage to compensate for uneven aging
