About This Documentation

This comprehensive guide provides everything you need to understand and implement the WIA Building Energy Management Standard (WIA-BEMS). Whether you're a building manager, energy engineer, system integrator, or software developer, this documentation will guide you through every aspect of creating energy-efficient, intelligent buildings.

The WIA-BEMS standard is designed to enable seamless integration of building energy management systems, reduce operational costs, minimize environmental impact, and support the transition to net-zero energy buildings.
