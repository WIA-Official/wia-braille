About This eBook

This comprehensive guide explores the Universal Metadata standard (WIA-CORE-008), a revolutionary approach to describing, organizing, and discovering digital content across all domains and industries. Whether you're a developer, information architect, data scientist, or content manager, this book provides the knowledge and practical tools you need to implement world-class metadata systems.
