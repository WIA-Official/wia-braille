Chapter 7: Integration & Interoperability

7.1 Court System Integration

Phase 4 of WIA-LEGAL-002 enables seamless integration with traditional court systems, allowing disputes to flow bidirectionally between ODR platforms and courts. This integration creates a continuum of dispute resolution options rather than isolated silos.

7.1.1 Integration Architecture

Integration Point Direction Purpose Data Exchange
Court-to-ODR Referral Court → ODR Divert suitable cases to mediation/arbitration Case details, party information, deadlines
ODR-to-Court Escalation ODR → Court Failed mediation escalates to litigation Full case history, evidence, communications
Settlement Filing ODR → Court Record settlement as consent order Settlement agreement, signatures, blockchain proof
Award Enforcement ODR → Court Convert arbitral award to court judgment Award, service proof, jurisdiction confirmation

Level Requirements Dispute Types
