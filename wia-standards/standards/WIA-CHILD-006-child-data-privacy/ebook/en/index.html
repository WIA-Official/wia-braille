About This Guide

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for WIA-CHILD-006: Child Data Privacy standard. This ebook provides in-depth coverage of child data protection principles, COPPA compliance requirements, data minimization strategies, and parental consent management systems.

Whether you're a developer implementing child privacy protections, a compliance officer ensuring regulatory adherence, or a parent seeking to understand digital safety, this guide offers practical insights, real-world examples, and actionable strategies.
