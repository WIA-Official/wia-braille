Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Virtual Fitness Standards.

This eBook explores the revolutionary intersection of virtual reality, augmented reality, and physical fitness. From immersive VR workouts to AI-powered coaching, discover how technology is transforming the way we exercise, train, and achieve our fitness goals.

Based on the WIA-IND-014 standard, this guide provides practical insights, technical implementations, and future-looking perspectives on virtual fitness ecosystems.
