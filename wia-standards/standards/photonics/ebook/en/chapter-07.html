Standards and Compliance

Regulatory compliance and adherence to industry standards ensure interoperability, safety, and market access across global markets.

Relevant Standards

  • International Standards: ISO 9001 (Quality Management), IEC 61508 (Functional Safety), ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security)
  • Industry-Specific: IEEE standards for specific domains, SEMI standards for semiconductor equipment, 3GPP for telecommunications
  • Regional Regulations: EU CE marking, FCC compliance (USA), CCC certification (China), regulatory requirements vary by market
  • Safety Standards: UL certification, CSA approval, TÜV certification for safety-critical applications
  • Environmental: RoHS compliance, REACH regulations, energy efficiency standards (Energy Star, etc.)

Certification Process

Obtaining necessary certifications requires systematic approach and substantial investment in testing and documentation.

  • Pre-assessment: Gap analysis against requirements, identify needed changes
  • Preparation: Implement required controls, generate documentation
  • Testing: Third-party testing labs, witness testing, design verification
  • Submission: Application, technical files, test reports
  • Review: Certification body review, potential requests for additional information
  • Certification: Certificate issuance, ongoing surveillance, periodic renewal
