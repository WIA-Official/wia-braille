WIA-DEF-013

NBC Defense Standard (Nuclear, Biological, Chemical)

弘益人間 · Benefit All Humanity

🔍 Overview

WIA-DEF-013 establishes comprehensive standards for Nuclear, Biological, and Chemical (NBC) defense systems, providing protection against weapons of mass destruction. This standard covers detection technologies, protective equipment, decontamination procedures, medical countermeasures, and integrated defense architectures for military and civilian applications in CBRN (Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear) threat environments.

💻 API Example

import { NBCDefense } from '@wia/def-013';

const nbc = new NBCDefense({
  mode: 'combat-ready',
  threatLevel: 'high',
  sensors: ['chemical', 'biological', 'radiological']
});

// Continuous monitoring
await nbc.startMonitoring({
  area: { lat: 35.1796, lon: 129.0756, radius: 5000 },
  frequency: 'real-time',
  alerts: ['immediate', 'threshold-exceeded']
});

// Detection event
nbc.on('detection', async (event) => {
  console.log(`ALERT: ${event.agent} detected`);
  console.log(`Concentration: ${event.concentration} ${event.unit}`);
  console.log(`Confidence: ${event.confidence}%`);

  if (event.threatLevel === 'immediate') {
    await nbc.initiateProtocol({
      action: 'MOPP-4',
      decontamination: 'prepare',
      medicalAlert: true,
      evacuation: event.concentration > event.lethalDose
    });
  }
});

// Hazard prediction
const hazard = await nbc.modelDispersion({
  releasePoint: { lat: 35.18, lon: 129.08 },
  agent: 'VX',
  quantity: 100, // kg
  weatherData: await nbc.getWeatherData(),
  terrain: 'urban'
});

console.log(`Affected area: ${hazard.affectedArea} km²`);
console.log(`Protective zones: ${JSON.stringify(hazard.zones)}`);
console.log(`Evacuation time: ${hazard.evacuationWindow} minutes`);

☣️ Threat Agents

Chemical Agents

  • Nerve Agents (G/V series): Sarin (GB), Soman (GD), Tabun (GA), VX - cholinesterase inhibitors causing rapid incapacitation
  • Blister Agents (H series): Sulfur mustard (HD), Nitrogen mustard (HN), Lewisite (L) - vesicants causing severe skin and respiratory damage
  • Blood Agents: Hydrogen cyanide (AC), Cyanogen chloride (CK) - cellular asphyxiants blocking oxygen utilization
  • Choking Agents: Chlorine (CL), Phosgene (CG) - pulmonary agents causing respiratory failure
  • Incapacitating Agents: BZ (3-quinuclidinyl benzilate) - anticholinergic causing temporary incapacitation

Biological Agents

  • Bacteria: Bacillus anthracis (anthrax), Yersinia pestis (plague), Francisella tularensis (tularemia), Brucella species (brucellosis)
  • Viruses: Variola major (smallpox), Filoviruses (Ebola, Marburg), Venezuelan equine encephalitis (VEE), Viral hemorrhagic fevers
  • Toxins: Botulinum toxin, Ricin, Staphylococcal enterotoxin B (SEB), T-2 mycotoxins
  • Emerging Threats: Genetically engineered pathogens, antibiotic-resistant strains, synthetic biology threats

Radiological & Nuclear

  • Alpha Emitters: Plutonium-239, Americium-241, Polonium-210 - high LET, internal hazard
  • Beta Emitters: Strontium-90, Cesium-137, Iodine-131 - skin and internal hazard
  • Gamma Sources: Cobalt-60, Cesium-137, Iridium-192 - external penetrating radiation
  • Nuclear Weapons: Fission, fusion, enhanced radiation (neutron bomb), electromagnetic pulse (EMP)
  • Radiological Dispersal Devices: "Dirty bombs" combining conventional explosives with radioactive materials

🚨 Deployment Scenarios

Scenario 1: Chemical Attack on Urban Center

  • Threat: Nerve agent (VX) dispersal in subway system
  • Detection: Automated chemical detectors trigger within 30 seconds
  • Response: MOPP-4 donned, area evacuation initiated, decontamination corridors established
  • Medical: Atropine/pralidoxime auto-injectors administered, mass casualty triage
  • Recovery: Technical decontamination of subway, environmental sampling, all-clear certification

Scenario 2: Biological Weapons Release

  • Threat: Aerosolized anthrax spores in government building
  • Detection: BioWatch air sampling detects Bacillus anthracis DNA within 12-36 hours
  • Response: Building quarantine, HEPA filtration, prophylactic antibiotics (ciprofloxacin/doxycycline)
  • Medical: Post-exposure prophylaxis for 60 days, anthrax vaccine adsorbed (AVA), monitoring for symptoms
  • Recovery: Fumigation with chlorine dioxide or formaldehyde, spore testing, environmental clearance

Scenario 3: Radiological Dispersal Device

  • Threat: Conventional explosive + Cesium-137 source (dirty bomb)
  • Detection: Radiation detectors identify elevated gamma radiation (>100 mR/hr)
  • Response: Hot zone established, radiation monitoring teams deployed, sheltering-in-place orders
  • Medical: Potassium iodide distribution (if radioiodine present), whole-body decontamination, bioassay sampling
  • Recovery: Debris removal, surface decontamination, long-term monitoring, risk communication

Scenario 4: Nuclear Weapon Detonation

  • Threat: Low-yield (10 kt) nuclear device in port city
  • Effects: Blast radius 1-2 km, thermal radiation 3-5 km, fallout pattern 10-50+ km downwind
  • Response: Immediate shelter, fallout protection (>10 halving thicknesses), exposure minimization
  • Medical: Acute radiation syndrome treatment, hematopoietic growth factors, stem cell transplant
  • Recovery: Dosimetry, contamination surveys, evacuation of hot zones, long-term health monitoring

🔐 Security & Compliance

  • NATO Standards: AEP-57 (CBRN Defense), AEP-58 (Chemical Detection), STANAG 4586 (UAV Systems)
  • US Military Standards: MIL-STD-282 (Individual Protection), MIL-PRF-32016 (Filter Canister), MIL-PRF-32052 (Detector)
  • Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC): Compliance with prohibition on development, production, stockpiling, and use
  • Biological Weapons Convention (BWC): Adherence to ban on biological warfare agents
  • IAEA Nuclear Security: Detection and response to illicit trafficking of nuclear materials
  • OSHA HAZWOPER: 29 CFR 1910.120 - Hazardous waste operations and emergency response training
  • NFPA 1994: Standard on protective ensembles for first responders to CBRN terrorism incidents
  • ISO 16933: Protective clothing against chemical agents - test methods and performance classification
  • Data Security: Encrypted NBC threat data transmission (AES-256), secure sensor networks, classified threat libraries
  • Export Control: ITAR Category XIV (Toxicological Agents), USML Category XIV, EAR ECCN 1C350-1C355

🔍 개요

WIA-DEF-013은 대량살상무기(WMD)에 대한 보호를 제공하는 핵·생물·화학(NBC) 방어 시스템에 대한 포괄적인 표준을 수립합니다. 이 표준은 감지 기술, 보호 장비, 제독 절차, 의료 대응책, 그리고 CBRN(화학, 생물학, 방사능, 핵) 위협 환경에서의 군사 및 민간 응용을 위한 통합 방어 아키텍처를 다룹니다.

☣️ 위협 작용제

화학 작용제

  • 신경 작용제 (G/V 계열): 사린(GB), 소만(GD), 타분(GA), VX - 콜린에스테라제 억제제로 신속한 무력화 유발
  • 수포 작용제 (H 계열): 황 겨자(HD), 질소 겨자(HN), 루이사이트(L) - 심각한 피부 및 호흡기 손상을 유발하는 수포제
  • 혈액 작용제: 시안화수소(AC), 염화시아노겐(CK) - 산소 이용을 차단하는 세포 질식제
  • 질식 작용제: 염소(CL), 포스겐(CG) - 호흡 부전을 유발하는 폐 작용제
  • 무력화 작용제: BZ - 일시적 무력화를 유발하는 항콜린제

생물학적 작용제

  • 박테리아: 탄저균, 페스트균, 야토병균, 브루셀라균
  • 바이러스: 천연두, 에볼라, 마버그, 베네수엘라 말 뇌염, 출혈열 바이러스
  • 독소: 보툴리눔 독소, 리신, 포도상구균 장독소 B, T-2 마이코톡신
  • 신흥 위협: 유전자 조작 병원체, 항생제 내성 균주, 합성 생물학 위협

방사능 및 핵

  • 알파 방출체: 플루토늄-239, 아메리슘-241, 폴로늄-210 - 높은 LET, 내부 위험
  • 베타 방출체: 스트론튬-90, 세슘-137, 요오드-131 - 피부 및 내부 위험
  • 감마 선원: 코발트-60, 세슘-137, 이리듐-192 - 외부 투과 방사선
  • 핵무기: 핵분열, 핵융합, 강화 방사선(중성자탄), 전자기 펄스(EMP)
  • 방사능 확산 장치: 방사성 물질과 재래식 폭발물을 결합한 "더티 밤"
