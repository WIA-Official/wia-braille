8.5 Conclusion

WIA-TIME-002 represents humanity's first comprehensive attempt to standardize the manipulation of spacetime itself. While practical implementation remains beyond current technology, the theoretical foundations are solid and the path forward is becoming clearer.

The challenges are immense—exotic matter generation, energy requirements, safety concerns—but they are engineering challenges, not fundamental barriers. With continued research and development, the technologies described in this standard may one day become as routine as electromagnetic manipulation is today.

The ultimate promise of spacetime manipulation is nothing less than freedom from the constraints that have bound humanity since our origins. The stars await, and the path to reach them lies through the mastery of spacetime itself.
