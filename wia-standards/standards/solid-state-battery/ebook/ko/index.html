About This Standard

The WIA-AUTO-028 standard was developed by the World Interoperability Association (WIA) to ensure consistent, secure, and interoperable implementations across the global Solid-State Battery ecosystem. Compliance with this standard demonstrates commitment to quality, security, and operational excellence.
