Chip Size Considerations

Smaller chips (<20μm) enable higher pixel density and resolution but require more precise transfer technology. Larger chips (40-50μm) are easier to handle with higher manufacturing yield.

Color Conversion vs RGB

Blue+CC approach uses single blue LED with quantum dot or phosphor conversion layer. RGB uses three separate LED chips. CC offers simpler assembly but lower color purity than native RGB.

Elastomer Stamp Transfer

Most widely used for large-area displays. PDMS stamp picks up thousands of chips simultaneously from donor wafer and transfers to TFT backplane. Yield typically 99.9%+.

Laser-Induced Forward Transfer (LIFT)

Laser pulse vaporizes sacrificial layer, propelling chip to target substrate. Highly accurate (±1μm) but slower throughput. Best for ultra-fine pitch displays.

Laser Repair Technology

Uses focused laser to remove defective LED and activate redundant pixel. Can fix dead pixels and some stuck pixels. Success rate 90-95%.

Redundant Pixel Design

Extra LEDs fabricated in each pixel cluster. When primary LED fails, redundant LED is activated electronically. Increases manufacturing yield from 95% to 99%+.
