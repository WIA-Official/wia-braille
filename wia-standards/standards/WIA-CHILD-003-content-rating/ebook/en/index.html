About This Guide

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for WIA-CHILD-003 Content Rating System. This standard provides a unified framework for age-appropriate content classification, real-time content monitoring, and child protection across digital platforms.

This ebook covers the complete implementation of content rating systems, from foundational concepts to advanced AI-powered analysis and global compliance. Each chapter provides detailed technical specifications, implementation examples, and practical guidance.
