Key Projects & Use Cases

⚡ Inter-Korean HVDC Link

765kV HVDC transmission line connecting Gangwon Province to Wonsan, enabling bi-directional power flow up to 2GW capacity. Supports renewable energy sharing and grid stabilization during peak demand periods.

🌊 Tidal Power Integration

Standardized protocols for integrating West Sea tidal power plants into the unified grid, leveraging Korea's exceptional tidal range for renewable energy generation and cross-border distribution.

☀️ Solar Farm Sharing

Unified solar energy distribution network connecting large-scale photovoltaic installations in the DMZ and rural areas with urban consumption centers, maximizing renewable capacity utilization.

💨 Wind Power Corridor

Integration of East Coast offshore wind farms with the unified grid, utilizing standardized protocols for variable renewable energy management and real-time balancing mechanisms.

🔋 Pumped Storage Coordination

Coordinated operation of pumped hydroelectric storage facilities across the peninsula for load balancing, peak shaving, and emergency power backup during grid instabilities.

🌐 Northeast Asia Supergrid

Foundation for regional power trading with China, Russia, Japan, and Mongolia, positioning the Korean Peninsula as a strategic energy hub in the Asia-Pacific energy market.
