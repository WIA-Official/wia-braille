# WIA-ENE-015: Zero Energy Building Standard 🏢

> **弘益人間 (홍익인간) - Benefit All Humanity**

## Overview

The WIA-ENE-015 Zero Energy Building Standard provides a comprehensive framework for designing, constructing, and operating buildings that produce as much energy as they consume on an annual basis. This standard combines passive design principles, high-efficiency systems, renewable energy generation, and intelligent building management to achieve net-zero energy performance.

## Standard Information

- **Standard ID:** WIA-ENE-015
- **Category:** Energy (ENE)
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Status:** Active
- **Organization:** World Certification Industry Association (WIA) / SmileStory Inc.
- **Release Date:** 2025

弘益人間 (홍익인간) - "Broadly benefit humanity"

## Key Features

### 🏗️ Passive House Design
- Ultra-low energy design principles
- Superior insulation (R-40+ walls, R-60+ roofs)
- Airtight construction (≤0.6 ACH50)
- High-performance windows (U ≤ 0.8 W/m²K)
- Thermal bridge-free detailing

### 💻 Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS)
- Real-time energy monitoring with 15-minute granularity
- Automated control of HVAC, lighting, and renewable systems
- Model Predictive Control (MPC) for optimization
- Fault Detection and Diagnostics (FDD)
- Demand response and grid integration

### ☀️ Renewable Energy Integration
- On-site solar PV systems (≥100% annual generation)
- Battery energy storage (≥20% daily solar production)
- Ground-source heat pumps for heating/cooling
- Solar thermal for domestic hot water
- Smart grid connectivity with bidirectional energy flow

### 📊 Energy Efficiency Standards
- Maximum EUI: 40-60 kWh/m²/year (by building type)
- Heating demand: ≤15 kWh/m²/year
- Cooling demand: ≤15 kWh/m²/year
- 100% LED lighting with controls
- ENERGY STAR appliances throughout

### 🌱 Green Building Certification
- Compatible with LEED, BREEAM, Living Building Challenge
- Four-phase certification process
- 12-24 months operational verification
- Performance-based certification
- Public case study contribution

### 🔬 Advanced Technologies
- Dynamic glass and smart windows
- Phase change materials (PCM) for thermal storage
- AI and machine learning optimization
- Digital twin integration
- Next-generation solar and battery technologies
- Blockchain for peer-to-peer energy trading

## Performance Requirements

### Building Envelope
- **Walls:** R-40 (U-0.15 W/m²K) minimum
- **Roof:** R-60 (U-0.10 W/m²K) minimum
- **Windows:** U-0.8 W/m²K maximum
- **Air Tightness:** ≤0.6 ACH50 verified by blower door test
- **Thermal Bridges:** Ψ-value ≤0.01 W/mK for all details

### Mechanical Systems
- **Heat Pumps:** SEER 18 / HSPF 10 minimum
- **Ventilation:** HRV/ERV with ≥75% heat recovery efficiency
- **Lighting:** 100% LED, ≥100 lumens/watt
- **Lighting Power Density:** 50% below ASHRAE 90.1

### Renewable Energy
- **Solar PV:** Generate ≥100% of annual consumption
- **Battery Storage:** ≥20% of daily solar generation or 4 hours critical loads
- **System Monitoring:** Real-time generation monitoring at 5-minute intervals
- **Grid Integration:** IEEE 1547-2018 compliant smart inverters

### Energy Performance
- **Net Annual Energy:** ≤0 kWh/year
- **Peak Heating Load:** ≤10 W/m²
- **Peak Cooling Load:** ≤10 W/m²
- **Embodied Carbon:** ≤500 kg CO2e/m² for new construction
- **Operational Carbon:** Net-zero annual emissions

## Certification Process

### Phase 1: Foundation & Design
- Energy modeling with PHPP or equivalent
- Envelope specifications and detailing
- HVAC and renewable system design
- Commissioning plan development
- **Result:** Design Certification (valid 3 years)

### Phase 2: System Integration
- BEMS architecture and monitoring design
- Renewable energy system integration
- Indoor environmental quality specifications
- Construction documentation
- **Result:** Construction Documentation Approval

### Phase 3: Implementation & Testing
- Progressive blower door testing
- Thermal imaging verification
- Systems commissioning
- Solar PV and battery testing
- Operator training
- **Result:** Construction Verification Certificate

### Phase 4: Certification & Operations
- 24-month operational monitoring
- Monthly energy balance verification
- Indoor environmental quality monitoring
- Occupant satisfaction surveys
- Performance optimization
- **Result:** WIA-ENE-015 Certification (Certified, Plus, or Exemplary)

## Case Studies

### Bullitt Center (Seattle, USA)
- **Type:** 6-story commercial office
- **Size:** 4,600 m²
- **Achievement:** 124% net positive energy
- **EUI:** 38 kWh/m²/year (83% below code)
- **Key:** 575 kW solar + geothermal + natural ventilation

### powerhouse Kjørbo (Norway)
- **Type:** Office retrofit
- **Size:** 3,800 m²
- **Achievement:** Net positive energy retrofit
- **Savings:** 90% energy reduction vs. pre-retrofit
- **Key:** 300mm wall insulation + 230kW solar

### SURE House (New Jersey, USA)
- **Type:** Single-family residence
- **Size:** 100 m²
- **Achievement:** 125% energy generation + hurricane resilience
- **Air Tightness:** 0.5 ACH50
- **Key:** SIPs construction + 10kW solar + 20kWh battery

### Shanghai Tower (China)
- **Type:** Super-tall mixed-use
- **Size:** 380,000 m² / 632m tall
- **Achievement:** 40% below typical supertall EUI
- **Key:** Double-skin facade + 200 wind turbines + geothermal
