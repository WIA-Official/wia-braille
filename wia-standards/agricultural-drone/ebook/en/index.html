  • Chapter 2: Multispectral Imaging & Remote Sensing
    Understanding multispectral cameras, bands (RGB, NIR, Red Edge), and remote sensing principles for agriculture.
  • Chapter 3: NDVI and Vegetation Health Analysis
    Deep dive into NDVI calculation, interpretation, and other vegetation indices. Crop health assessment techniques.
  • Chapter 4: Precision Crop Spraying
    Spray system design, nozzle types, application rates, spray drift prevention, and regulatory compliance.
  • Chapter 5: Mission Planning & Flight Operations
    Flight planning software, waypoint generation, weather considerations, and safe flight operations.
  • Chapter 6: Data Processing & Analysis
    Image stitching, orthomosaic generation, GIS integration, and agricultural data analytics.
  • Chapter 7: Integration with Farm Management
