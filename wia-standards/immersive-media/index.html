Core Features

Transform education with immersive experiences

🥽

VR/AR/XR Integration

Full support for virtual reality, augmented reality, and extended reality devices. Create fully immersive learning environments that transcend physical boundaries.

🎥

360° Video

Capture and deliver 360-degree video content for immersive educational experiences. Students can explore any environment from all angles.

📐

3D Visualization

Render complex 3D models, scientific visualizations, and interactive simulations. Make abstract concepts tangible and explorable.

🎮

Haptic Feedback

Integrate touch and force feedback for truly immersive learning. Feel textures, forces, and interactions in virtual environments.

🔊

Spatial Audio

3D positional audio that creates realistic soundscapes. Hear sounds from their actual location in virtual space for enhanced immersion.

📖

Immersive Storytelling

Narrative-driven learning experiences where students become active participants in educational stories and scenarios.

🧪

Virtual Labs

Conduct experiments in safe, controlled virtual environments. Explore dangerous or expensive scenarios without real-world risks.

🌍

Virtual Field Trips

Transport students anywhere - from ancient civilizations to outer space. Experience locations impossible or impractical to visit physically.

📊

Learning Analytics

Track engagement, interaction patterns, and learning outcomes. Understand how students interact with immersive content.

🔄

Cross-Platform

Support for VR headsets, AR glasses, mobile devices, and desktop browsers. Deliver immersive content to any device.

⚡

Real-Time Rendering

High-performance graphics and physics engines for smooth, responsive immersive experiences at 90+ FPS.

🎨

Content Creation Tools

Intuitive tools for educators to create their own immersive content without programming expertise.

弘益人間

Our immersive media standard is built on the Korean philosophy of 弘益人間 (홍익인간) - "Benefit All Humanity". We believe that immersive technologies should democratize access to transformative educational experiences, making world-class learning available to students everywhere, regardless of their physical location or economic circumstances.

Through VR/AR/XR, we can transport students to ancient Rome, inside a human cell, or to the surface of Mars - creating memories and understanding that last a lifetime.

Use Cases

Real-world applications of immersive media in education

🏛️ Historical Reconstruction

Step inside ancient civilizations and historical events. Experience history as if you were there, walking through ancient Rome or witnessing key historical moments.

🔬 Scientific Exploration

Journey inside molecules, explore the solar system, or dive deep into ocean trenches. Visualize phenomena impossible to observe in traditional settings.

🏥 Medical Training

Practice surgical procedures, explore human anatomy in 3D, and train for emergency scenarios in risk-free virtual environments.

🏗️ Architecture & Engineering

Design and walk through buildings before they're built. Visualize engineering concepts and structural principles in interactive 3D.

🎭 Arts & Culture

Visit world-class museums, attend virtual performances, and experience cultural heritage sites from anywhere in the world.

🚀 Space Exploration

Train like astronauts, explore the International Space Station, and experience spacewalks - all from the classroom.
