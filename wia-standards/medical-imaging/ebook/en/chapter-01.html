Overview

DICOM (Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine) is the international standard for medical imaging data. Developed in 1993 as DICOM 3.0, it enables seamless communication between different manufacturers' medical imaging equipment and systems.

Key Topics Covered

This chapter covers the following essential topics:

  • History and evolution of DICOM standards
  • DICOM architecture and information models
  • Service classes (Storage, Query/Retrieve, Worklist, etc.)
  • Data elements and tag structure
  • Value Representation (VR) types
  • Network communication protocols
  • AE Titles and DICOM associations
  • Security mechanisms (TLS, digital signatures)
  • Global adoption and compliance

Market Impact

The global medical imaging market exceeds $500 billion, with DICOM serving as the universal standard adopted by all major manufacturers. Over 100% of modern imaging equipment supports DICOM, making it the de facto standard worldwide.
