# CRYO-REVIVAL: Revival Protocol Standards

> "홍익인간" (弘益人間) - Benefit All Humanity

## Overview

CRYO-REVIVAL defines comprehensive standards for the safe revival of cryopreserved individuals. These standards cover the technical procedures, medical requirements, personnel qualifications, and contingency protocols for bringing preserved subjects back to life.

## Core Domains

### 1. Revival Preparation
- Pre-revival assessment
- Facility readiness verification
- Team assembly and briefing
- Equipment calibration

### 2. Thawing Protocol
- Temperature gradient management
- Cryoprotectant removal
- Tissue rehydration
- Cellular monitoring

### 3. Medical Intervention
- Cardiovascular restart
- Respiratory support
- Neurological assessment
- Organ function restoration

### 4. Post-Revival Care
- Intensive monitoring
- Rehabilitation protocols
- Psychological support
- Social reintegration

## Safety Requirements

### Critical Thresholds
- Temperature variance: ±0.1°C maximum
- Warming rate: 50-200°C/min (stage-dependent)
- CPA removal time: 4-8 hours
- Cardiac restart window: 30 minutes

### Failure Protocols
- Emergency re-cooling procedures
- Damage assessment protocols
- Re-preservation decision criteria
- Family notification procedures

## Personnel Requirements

### Revival Team
- Revival Director (1)
- Cryonics Specialists (2-4)
- Cardiovascular Surgeons (2)
- Neurologists (1-2)
- Anesthesiologists (2)
- ICU Nurses (4-6)
- Support Staff (4-8)

### Qualifications
- WIA Revival Certification required
- Minimum 5 years relevant experience
- Annual recertification mandatory
- Simulation training completed
