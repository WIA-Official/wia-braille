The WIA Urban Forest Creation Standard provides a comprehensive framework for transforming cities through strategic tree planting, forest corridor development, and ecosystem restoration.

About This Ebook

This official ebook provides a comprehensive guide to the WIA Urban Forest Creation Standard. It covers the complete specification for urban tree planting, forest health monitoring, carbon sequestration tracking, and biodiversity enhancement in cities worldwide.

Get Certified

After reading this ebook, consider official WIA Urban Forest certification:

Certification Level For Cost
Level 1: Basic Forest Small urban parks (10-50 trees) $300
Level 2: District Forest District-level programs (50-500 trees) $1,500
Level 3: City Forest City-wide programs (500+ trees) $5,000
Level 4: Regional Network Multi-city coordination $15,000

Note: The specification and SDKs are FREE. Certification is optional but recommended for verified carbon credits.
