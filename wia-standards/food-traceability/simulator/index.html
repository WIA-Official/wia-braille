    📡 GS1 EPCIS Protocol
    
            
    Implement GS1 Electronic Product Code Information Services (EPCIS) for standardized event capture.
    

            
    
                
    
                    
    ObjectEvent
    
                    
    Records what happened to objects at a specific time and place (e.g., harvesting, packing)
    
                
    
                
    
                    
    AggregationEvent
    
                    
    Records parent-child relationships (e.g., boxes packed into pallet)
    
                
    
                
    
                    
    TransformationEvent
    
                    
    Records when inputs are transformed into outputs (e.g., apples to juice)
    
                
    
                
    
                    
    TransactionEvent
    
                    
    Records business transactions like purchase orders and shipments
