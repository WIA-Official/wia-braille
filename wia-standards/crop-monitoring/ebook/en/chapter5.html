Chapter 5: Yield Prediction & Forecasting

5.1 Why Yield Prediction Matters

Accurate yield forecasts enable:

  • Market Planning: Pre-sell crops at optimal prices
  • Logistics: Arrange harvest labor and equipment
  • Insurance: Verify coverage and file claims
  • Financial Planning: Predict revenue, manage cash flow

5.2 Prediction Inputs

Growth Metrics

Inputs for Yield Model:
- Plant height over time
- Leaf Area Index (LAI)
- NDVI from satellite/drone imagery
- Growth stage progression (BBCH codes)
- Biomass accumulation

Environmental Data

  • Temperature (growing degree days)
  • Rainfall (cumulative, timing)
  • Solar radiation
  • Soil moisture

Historical Data

  • Past yields for this field
  • Variety performance
  • Management practices

5.3 Prediction Models

Machine Learning Approach

import numpy as np
from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestRegressor

# Features: height, LAI, chlorophyll, temp, rainfall
X_train = [[45, 3.8, 42, 25, 500], ...]
y_train = [5500, ...]  # Actual yields

model = RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=100)
model.fit(X_train, y_train)

# Predict yield for current crop
current_data = [[50, 4.2, 45, 26, 520]]
predicted_yield = model.predict(current_data)
print(f"Estimated yield: {predicted_yield[0]} kg/ha")

Crop Simulation Models

Process-based models like DSSAT, APSIM simulate daily crop growth using physiological equations.

5.5 Scenario Analysis

Test "what-if" scenarios to optimize management decisions.

POST /forecast/scenario

{
  "cropId": "CROP-2025-001",
  "scenario": {
    "weatherOverride": {
      "rainfall": "+20%",
      "temperature": "+2°C"
    },
    "interventions": ["extra_fertilizer", "pest_control"]
  }
}

Response:
{
  "baselineYield": 5500,
  "scenarioYield": 6200,
  "difference": "+12.7%",
  "recommendation": "Investment in interventions justified"
}

📊 Prediction Accuracy

Early season (30 days after planting): ±25% accuracy
Mid season (60 days): ±15% accuracy
Late season (2 weeks before harvest): ±5% accuracy

5.6 Real-World Application

Case Study: Corn farmers in Brazil used yield predictions to negotiate forward contracts. Accurate forecasts (within 8% of actual) allowed them to lock in prices 3 months early, increasing revenue by $150/hectare vs. spot market sales.
