Chapter 4: AI Disease Detection

4.1 Computer Vision for Agriculture

AI-powered disease detection uses convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to identify plant diseases from images. Trained on thousands of labeled images, these models achieve 85-95% accuracy.

Common Crop Diseases

  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Affects tomatoes, potatoes. Brown lesions on leaves.
  • Rust Diseases: Fungal infections causing orange/yellow spots.
  • Powdery Mildew: White powder-like coating on leaves.
  • Bacterial Spot: Dark, water-soaked lesions.

📸 Best Practices for Image Capture

  • Natural daylight, avoid direct sun glare
  • Focus on affected leaves, 10-30cm distance
  • Multiple angles, close-ups of lesions
  • Capture progression over days for better diagnosis

4.4 Pest Detection

Similar techniques detect pests like aphids, armyworms, and beetles. Object detection models (YOLO, Faster R-CNN) count pests and track infestations.

4.5 Real-World Impact

Case Study: Tomato farm in California reduced late blight losses by 80% using AI detection. Early warnings (detected at 8% infection vs. 35% manual) allowed preventive treatment.
