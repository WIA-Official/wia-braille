2.2 Feeding Station

Feeding Station Features
========================

Basic Features:
  - Portion-controlled dispensing
  - Scheduled feeding times
  - Food level monitoring
  - Bowl presence detection

Advanced Features:
  - Pet recognition (RFID/Microchip/Facial)
  - Multi-pet support
  - Consumption tracking
  - Weight measurement
  - Slow-feed modes
  - Fresh food storage
  - Connected water fountain

Specifications:
  Capacity: 2-10 kg dry food
  Accuracy: +/- 5% portion
  Power: AC with battery backup
  Noise: < 45 dB operation

2.3 Companion Robot

Companion Robot Features
========================

Physical:
  - Autonomous navigation
  - Safe collision avoidance
  - Pet-friendly form factor
  - Stable movement

Interactive:
  - Two-way audio/video
  - Treat dispensing
  - Play attachments
  - Response to commands

Intelligence:
  - Pet behavior learning
  - Routine adaptation
  - Emotion detection
  - Activity tracking

Specifications:
  Battery: 4-8 hours active
  Speed: 0.3-0.5 m/s max
  Height: 20-40 cm typical
  Weight: 2-5 kg

2.5 Monitoring Station

Environment Sensors
===================

Climate:
  - Temperature (0.1 C accuracy)
  - Humidity (2% accuracy)
  - Air pressure

Air Quality:
  - Particulate matter (PM2.5)
  - VOC levels
  - CO2 concentration
  - Ammonia (litter areas)

Activity:
  - Motion detection
  - Sound level
  - Light intensity

Optional:
  - Camera (local/cloud)
  - Microphone
  - Weight pad

2.9 Hardware Standards

Material Requirements
=====================

Pet-Safe Materials:
  - Food-grade plastics (BPA-free)
  - Stainless steel (food contact)
  - Non-toxic coatings
  - Rounded edges

Electrical Safety:
  - Low voltage DC operation
  - Cord protection
  - Water resistance (IPX4+)
  - Overcurrent protection

Mechanical Safety:
  - No pinch points
  - Stable base
  - Breakaway attachments
  - Motor torque limiting
