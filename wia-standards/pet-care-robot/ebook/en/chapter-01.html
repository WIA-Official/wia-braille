1.1 Purpose of Care Robots

Pet care robots serve as extensions of human caregiving, providing automated assistance with essential care tasks while maintaining the bond between pets and their families.

Core Objectives

Objective Description Benefit
Consistent Care Regular feeding and watering Proper nutrition timing
Enrichment Interactive play and stimulation Mental and physical health
Monitoring Health and environment tracking Early problem detection
Connection Remote interaction capability Maintain bond when away
Safety Pet-safe operation Peace of mind

1.4 Design Principles

Pet-First Design

Design Priority Order
=====================

1. Pet Safety
   - Non-toxic materials
   - No pinch points
   - Emergency stop capability
   - Gentle operation

2. Pet Comfort
   - Low noise operation (<50dB)
   - Non-threatening appearance
   - Predictable behavior
   - Gradual introduction

3. Pet Health
   - Accurate portion control
   - Clean operation
   - Health monitoring
   - Pattern detection

4. Owner Convenience
   - Easy setup
   - Remote access
   - Clear status
   - Low maintenance
