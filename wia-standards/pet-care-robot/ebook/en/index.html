About This Standard

The WIA-PET-CARE-ROBOT standard defines protocols for robotic systems that provide automated pet care. These systems enhance pet wellbeing through consistent feeding, interactive enrichment, environmental monitoring, and health tracking while maintaining the highest safety standards.

Key Features

  • [i] Automated feeding with portion control and pet recognition
  • [i] Interactive play systems with AI adaptation
  • [i] Environmental monitoring for optimal comfort
  • [i] Health metric tracking and pattern detection
  • [i] Comprehensive safety protocols
  • [i] Remote monitoring and control capabilities
  • [i] Multi-pet household support
  • [i] Integration with WIA health standards
