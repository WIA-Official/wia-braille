5.2 Comfort Zones

Species-Specific Comfort Ranges
===============================

Dogs:
  Temperature: 18-24 C (optimal 21 C)
  Humidity: 30-70% (optimal 50%)
  Noise: < 85 dB (comfortable < 60 dB)

Cats:
  Temperature: 20-26 C (optimal 24 C)
  Humidity: 30-70% (optimal 50%)
  Noise: < 80 dB (comfortable < 50 dB)

Small Mammals:
  Temperature: 18-24 C (species specific)
  Humidity: 40-60%
  Noise: < 70 dB

Birds:
  Temperature: 18-27 C (species specific)
  Humidity: 40-70%
  Noise: Variable (some prefer quiet)

5.3 Alert Configuration

Alert Thresholds

Parameter Warning Critical Action
Temperature High > 28 C > 32 C Notify, suggest cooling
Temperature Low < 16 C < 10 C Notify, suggest heating
Humidity High > 70% > 80% Notify, mold risk
Humidity Low < 25% < 15% Notify, suggest humidifier
Air Quality > 100 AQI > 150 AQI Alert, ventilate
Ammonia > 15 PPM > 25 PPM Clean litter, ventilate

5.7 Automation Rules

Example Automation Rules
========================

Rule: "Keep Cool"
  IF temperature > 26 C
  AND pet_present = true
  THEN
    turn_on(smart_fan)
    notify("Room getting warm for pet")

Rule: "Fresh Air"
  IF air_quality > 80 AQI
  AND outdoor_quality < 50 AQI
  THEN
    notify("Consider opening windows")

Rule: "Litter Alert"
  IF ammonia > 20 PPM
  THEN
    send_alert("Litter box needs cleaning")
    increase_circulation()

Rule: "Night Mode"
  IF time_between(22:00, 06:00)
  AND light_level > 100 lux
  THEN
    dim_lights(50%)
    notify("Bright lights may disturb pet sleep")

5.8 Air Quality Management

Air Quality Index Interpretation
================================

AQI 0-50: Good
  - Normal activity
  - No concerns

AQI 51-100: Moderate
  - Sensitive pets may be affected
  - Monitor for symptoms

AQI 101-150: Unhealthy for Sensitive
  - Limit outdoor access
  - Consider air purifier
  - Monitor closely

AQI 151-200: Unhealthy
  - Keep indoors
  - Run air purifier
  - Alert owner

AQI 201+: Very Unhealthy
  - Emergency alert
  - Maximize air filtration
  - Consider relocation

5.9 Noise Monitoring

Noise Level Guidelines
======================

< 40 dB: Quiet
  - Ideal for rest
  - Good for anxious pets

40-60 dB: Moderate
  - Normal household
  - Acceptable for most pets

60-80 dB: Loud
  - May cause stress
  - Monitor pet behavior

> 80 dB: Very Loud
  - Can cause fear
  - Alert owner
  - Check source

Noise Events:
  - Log sudden loud noises
  - Correlate with pet behavior
  - Identify recurring patterns
  - Fireworks/thunder detection

5.10 Lighting Cycles

Light Level Management
======================

Natural Light Cycle:
  Dawn (06:00-08:00): Gradual increase
  Day (08:00-18:00): 300-500 lux
  Dusk (18:00-20:00): Gradual decrease
  Night (20:00-06:00): < 50 lux

Recommendations:
  - Maintain consistent schedule
  - Avoid sudden light changes
  - Night light for anxious pets
  - Seasonal adjustment

Integration:
  - Smart bulb control
  - Curtain automation
  - Wake-up simulation
  - Calming mode (dimmed)
