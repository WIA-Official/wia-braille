About This Ebook

This comprehensive guide covers all aspects of medical device cybersecurity, including FDA guidance, pacemaker and insulin pump security, network segmentation, firmware updates, penetration testing, incident response, and legacy device protection.

Each chapter provides practical, implementable knowledge with real-world examples to help healthcare organizations and manufacturers protect medical devices and ensure patient safety.

Chapter 1
FDA Cybersecurity Guidance
Learn FDA's medical device cybersecurity guidance, premarket and postmarket requirements, and risk management frameworks.
Chapter 2
Pacemaker Security
Understand security threats and defense strategies for pacemakers and cardiac implantable electronic devices (CIED).
Chapter 3
Insulin Pump Vulnerabilities
Master cybersecurity risks and mitigation methods for insulin pumps and continuous glucose monitoring systems.
Chapter 4
Network Segmentation
Learn medical network segmentation architecture, VLAN design, firewall policies, and zero trust principles.
Chapter 5
Firmware Update Management
Implement secure firmware update processes, secure boot, code signing, and rollback mechanisms.
Chapter 6
Penetration Testing
Execute medical device penetration testing methodologies, vulnerability assessment, ethical hacking, and reporting.
Chapter 7
Incident Response
Build medical device security incident response plans, detection, containment, recovery, and post-incident analysis.
Chapter 8
Legacy Device Protection
Assess security risks of legacy medical devices and apply compensating controls, network isolation, and replacement strategies.
