Introduction

This chapter covers essential aspects of airspace management including LAANC and geofencing UTM communication protocols and standards Detect and Avoid systems for collision prevention cybersecurity and data protection in UTM.

Airspace Classification

Unrestricted airspace allows free flight with basic compliance. Controlled airspace requires prior authorization. No-Fly Zones (NFZ) prohibit all drone operations. Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFR) create temporary no-fly areas for events or emergencies.

LAANC System

Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability (LAANC) revolutionized drone approvals in the USA. Previously taking 90 days, approvals now occur in seconds. LAANC divides airspace around airports into grids with maximum altitude limits, processes requests automatically, and notifies air traffic control in real-time.

Geofencing Technology

Hard geofences physically prevent entry through autopilot intervention. Soft geofences warn but allow passage. Dynamic geofences change based on time or events. Altitude-based limits restrict vertical flight. Time-based restrictions apply during specific hours.

Common Geofenced Areas

Airports (5-10km radius), military facilities, power plants (especially nuclear), government buildings, prisons, and national borders all typically have geofences. Major events like Olympics create temporary geofenced zones.

Dynamic Airspace Management

UTM manages airspace density in real-time, limiting drone numbers per volume. Weather integration closes airspace during storms, high winds, or poor visibility. Emergency priority immediately clears airspace for first responders. Traffic optimization suggests alternate routes during congestion.

Airspace Reservation

Immediate flight (real-time), short-term (1-24 hours ahead), recurring (weekly/monthly), and long-term exclusive reservations enable different operational models. Allocation uses first-come-first-served, priority-based, auction, or AI optimization algorithms.
