Chapter 6

🛡️ Detect and Avoid 🔒 UTM Security

Introduction

This chapter covers essential aspects of Detect and Avoid systems for collision prevention cybersecurity and data protection in UTM.

DAA Overview

Detect and Avoid (DAA) is the automated equivalent of "see and avoid" for manned aircraft. DAA systems use sensors to detect obstacles, assess collision risk, and execute avoidance maneuvers. This multi-layered approach combines strategic, tactical, and emergency avoidance.

Sensor Technologies

Radar provides all-weather long-range detection. LiDAR offers precise 3D mapping. Optical cameras are cost-effective for visual detection. Infrared enables night operations. ADS-B receivers detect cooperative aircraft. Acoustic sensors provide omnidirectional awareness. Sensor fusion combines multiple inputs for reliability.

Collision Risk Assessment

Time to Closest Point of Approach (TCPA) calculates when objects will be nearest. Distance at Closest Point of Approach (DCPA) determines minimum separation. Well Clear standards define safe distances: 100m horizontal (drone-drone), 500m (drone-aircraft), 50m vertical (drone-drone), 150m (drone-aircraft).

Avoidance Strategies

Strategic deconfliction prevents conflicts during flight planning. Tactical deconfliction adjusts routes in real-time through speed changes, altitude shifts, or path alterations. Emergency avoidance executes immediate maneuvers: vertical climb/descent, hard turns, immediate hover, or emergency landing.

Right-of-Way Rules

Head-on encounters require both drones to turn right. In crossing situations, the drone on the right has priority. Overtaking drones must pass on the right. Landing aircraft have absolute priority. Emergency vehicles override all others. Manned aircraft always have priority over drones.

Cooperative vs Non-Cooperative

Cooperative avoidance occurs when both aircraft communicate through USS coordination. Non-cooperative targets (birds, unlawful drones, debris) require sensor-only detection with conservative avoidance margins.

Performance Requirements

Detection range: minimum 500m (drone-drone), 2km (aircraft). Position accuracy < 5m, speed accuracy < 1 m/s. Update rate ≥ 10 Hz. Reaction time < 1 second from detection to maneuver. False positive rate < 1%, false negative rate < 0.001%. Availability > 99.9%.
