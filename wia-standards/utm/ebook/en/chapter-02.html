🏗️ UTM Architecture

2.1 Distributed Architecture

UTM employs a distributed architecture where multiple stakeholders cooperate. Unlike centralized ATM, UTM distributes responsibility among government agencies, private companies, and drone operators. This design ensures scalability, resilience, and innovation.

2.2 FIMS: Flight Information Management System

FIMS is the government-operated system that serves as the regulatory backbone of UTM. Operated by aviation authorities, FIMS manages airspace information, approves flights, enforces regulations, integrates manned aircraft data, handles emergencies, and collects operational data.

FIMS provides critical data to USS including no-fly zones, temporary flight restrictions, emergency closures, and manned aircraft positions. It receives flight plans, position reports, incident reports, and operational statistics from USS.

2.3 USS: UAS Service Supplier

USS is a private sector innovation in UTM. Commercial USS providers offer services to drone operators including flight planning, real-time monitoring, collision avoidance support, and information services (weather, airspace, terrain).

Multiple USS can operate simultaneously, creating a competitive marketplace that drives innovation and efficiency. Key USS providers include AirMap, Altitude Angel, OneSky, Unifly, and Skyward (Verizon).

2.4 USS-to-USS Communication

USS systems must communicate with each other to coordinate operations. The USS-to-USS (U2U) protocol enables Operation Intent Sharing, conflict detection and resolution, airspace reservation, and emergency coordination.

2.5 PSU: Public Safety USS

PSU is a specialized USS dedicated to public safety and emergency response. PSU has enhanced privileges including immediate airspace access, priority override authority, real-time surveillance, forced landing capability, and law enforcement integration.

2.6 Data Flows

During flight planning, operators submit plans to USS, which validates feasibility, coordinates with other USS, and submits to FIMS for approval. During flight, drones broadcast position via Remote ID, USS tracks compliance, and reports to FIMS. Post-flight, USS generates flight records and releases airspace reservations.
