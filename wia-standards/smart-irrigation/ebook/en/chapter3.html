Drip Irrigation Systems

3.1 Introduction to Drip Irrigation

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the plant root zone through a network of pipes, tubes, and emitters. With 85-95% efficiency, it's the most water-efficient irrigation method available.

3.2 System Components

Main Line & Submains

  • PVC or HDPE pipes (25-100mm diameter)
  • Pressure rating: 4-10 bar
  • Buried or surface installation

Lateral Lines

  • Polyethylene tubing (12-20mm diameter)
  • Pressure-compensating or non-compensating
  • Spacing: 0.5-2.0 meters between laterals

Emitters

  • Inline emitters: Built into drip tape/tubing
  • Online emitters: Inserted into lateral lines
  • Micro-sprayers: For larger coverage areas
  • Flow rates: 0.5-8 liters per hour

Filtration

  • Screen filters (120-200 mesh)
  • Disk filters for high sediment loads
  • Sand media filters for surface water
  • Automatic backflushing systems

3.3 Design Principles

Emitter Spacing

  • Row crops: 0.3-0.6m spacing
  • Vegetables: 0.2-0.4m spacing
  • Orchards: 1-2 emitters per tree
  • Overlap wetting patterns for uniform coverage

Hydraulic Design

Pressure Loss Calculation:

ΔP = f × (L/D) × (v²/2g)
Where:
ΔP = pressure loss (kPa)
f = friction factor
L = pipe length (m)
D = pipe diameter (m)
v = flow velocity (m/s)
g = gravity (9.81 m/s²)

3.4 Automation & Control

Valve Actuation

  • Solenoid valves (24VAC or latching)
  • Normally closed configuration
  • Flow control valves for precise delivery
  • Zone isolation for independent control

Controllers

  • Multi-zone programmable controllers
  • Weather-based smart controllers
  • Soil moisture sensor integration
  • Remote access via cellular/WiFi

3.5 Fertigation

Injecting fertilizers through drip systems (fertigation) improves nutrient use efficiency:

  • Venturi injectors: No external power required
  • Injection pumps: Precise dosing control
  • Fertilizer tanks: Pressure differential injection
  • Typical injection rates: 0.5-2% of flow

3.6 Maintenance

Regular Tasks:

  1. Flush lateral lines monthly
  2. Clean filters weekly (or per pressure differential)
  3. Inspect emitters for clogging
  4. Check for leaks and damage
  5. Acid treatment for mineral deposits (quarterly)
  6. Chlorination for biological growth (as needed)
