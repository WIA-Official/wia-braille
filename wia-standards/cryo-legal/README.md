# CRYO-LEGAL

Legal Status Standards for Cryopreserved Individuals

## Overview

CRYO-LEGAL defines standards for managing the legal status of cryopreserved individuals, including death declaration handling, legal personhood during preservation, rights restoration upon revival, and integration with existing legal frameworks.

## Legal Status States

```
LIVING -> LEGALLY_DECEASED -> PRESERVED_STATUS -> REVIVAL_PENDING -> LEGALLY_RESTORED
                                    |
                                    v
                            PERMANENT_DEATH
```

## Key Components

### Legal Status Categories

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| LEGALLY_DECEASED | Traditional death certificate issued |
| PRESERVED_STATUS | Special legal status during cryopreservation |
| REVIVAL_PENDING | Revival procedure initiated |
| LEGALLY_RESTORED | Full legal personhood restored |
| PERMANENT_DEATH | Revival failed, final death |

### Document Types

- Death Certificate (Original)
- Preservation Status Declaration
- Legal Status Suspension Order
- Revival Notification
- Legal Restoration Certificate
- Identity Verification Order

### Jurisdiction Handling

- Multi-jurisdiction recognition
- International treaty compliance
- Conflict of laws resolution
- Extraterritorial effects

## Integration Points

- **CRYO-CONSENT**: Consent verification for legal actions
- **CRYO-IDENTITY**: Identity verification for restoration
- **CRYO-REVIVAL**: Revival status triggers legal restoration
- **CRYO-ASSET**: Asset restoration upon legal restoration

## Philosophy

> "Hongik Ingan" - Benefit All Humanity
>
> Legal frameworks must evolve to accommodate the possibility of life extension through cryopreservation. These standards ensure that preserved individuals retain appropriate legal protections and can be fully restored to legal personhood upon successful revival.
