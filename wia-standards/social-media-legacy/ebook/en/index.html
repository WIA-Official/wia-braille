📱 Social Media Legacy Standard

A Comprehensive Guide to Digital Afterlife Management

WIA-LEG-007 v2.0

About This Book

Standard: WIA-LEG-007

Version: 2.0

Philosophy: 弘益人間 (Hongik Ingan) - Benefit All Humanity

Purpose: To provide a comprehensive framework for managing social media accounts after death, preserving digital memories, and honoring the deceased's wishes while respecting privacy and legal requirements.

Audience: Platform developers, legal professionals, families, legacy planners, and anyone interested in digital estate management.

Table of Contents

CHAPTER 1
Introduction to Digital Legacy
Understanding the importance of digital afterlife planning, historical context of social media legacy, and the philosophical foundations of 弘益人間 in digital preservation.
CHAPTER 2
The Four-Phase Protocol
Detailed exploration of Preparation, Verification, Execution, and Maintenance phases. Learn how each phase ensures secure, respectful, and legally compliant legacy management.
CHAPTER 3
Memorialization and Tribute Pages
Creating meaningful digital tributes, managing memorial accounts, privacy settings, and best practices for honoring the deceased while supporting the grieving community.
CHAPTER 4
Legacy Contacts and Permissions
Designating trusted individuals, defining permissions, legal considerations, and managing the transition of account control. Includes templates and checklists.
CHAPTER 5
Data Export and Preservation
Complete guide to downloading, archiving, and preserving social media data. Covers formats (JSON, XML, CSV), storage strategies, and long-term preservation techniques.
CHAPTER 6
AI Memorial Bots
Revolutionary technology for creating interactive memorial chatbots. Learn about personality modeling, training data, ethical considerations, and implementation strategies.
CHAPTER 7
Legal and Privacy Frameworks
GDPR compliance, death verification processes, privacy laws across jurisdictions, and handling disputes. Essential reading for legal professionals and platform operators.
CHAPTER 8
Implementation and Future Directions
Platform integration guides, API specifications, case studies, and emerging trends in digital legacy management. Roadmap for the next decade of social media afterlife.
