Chapter 1: Introduction to IoMT

"Connected medical devices are not just technology. They are life-saving systems that improve patient outcomes and amplify healthcare capabilities."

1.1 What is IoMT?

IoMT (Internet of Medical Things) refers to the ecosystem of medical devices and applications connected to the internet and healthcare IT systems for collecting, analyzing, and transmitting data.

IoMT enables:

  • Real-time monitoring — Track patient health 24/7
  • Telemedicine — Healthcare delivery regardless of distance
  • Predictive analytics — AI-based disease prediction
  • Personalized treatment — Tailored care based on individual data
  • Automated workflows — Optimized medical processes

1.2 Market Outlook

The medical IoT market is experiencing explosive growth:

  • $158B — Global IoMT market size in 2022
  • $289B — Projected market size by 2028
  • 10.5% — CAGR 2023-2028
  • 500M+ — Connected medical devices by 2030

For Healthcare Providers
