📄 WIA-SOC-013: Public Document Standard

弘益人間 (Hongik Ingan) - Benefit All Humanity

Universal document standards for global recognition

This comprehensive guide covers the WIA-SOC-013 Public Document Standard, defining international protocols for document digitization, archival standards, metadata schemas, access control, digital signatures, and cross-border recognition.

📚 Table of Contents

  1. Chapter 01: Introduction to Public Document Digitization

    Overview of public document challenges, digitization benefits, international standards landscape, and the WIA-SOC-013 framework.

  2. Chapter 02: Document Digitization Technologies

    OCR engines, image processing, quality enhancement, format conversion, and automated metadata extraction techniques.

  3. Chapter 03: Archival Standards and Long-Term Preservation

    PDF/A formats, TIFF specifications, migration strategies, and ensuring document accessibility over decades.

  4. Chapter 04: Metadata Schemas and Classification

    Dublin Core, PREMIS, custom taxonomies, and standardized metadata for document discovery and management.

  5. Chapter 05: Digital Signatures and PKI Infrastructure

    ECDSA, RSA signatures, certificate authorities, timestamp services, and ensuring document authenticity.

  6. Chapter 06: Access Control and Privacy Protection

    RBAC, ABAC policies, encryption, data minimization, and compliance with GDPR and privacy regulations.

  7. Chapter 07: Cross-Border Recognition and Interoperability

    International agreements, apostille services, mutual recognition frameworks, and multilingual support.

  8. Chapter 08: Implementation and Real-World Applications

    Case studies, deployment strategies, integration guides, and best practices for production systems.
