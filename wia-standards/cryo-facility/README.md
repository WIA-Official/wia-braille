# CRYO-FACILITY

냉동보존 시설 인증 표준 (Cryopreservation Facility Certification Standard)

## Overview

CRYO-FACILITY establishes certification standards for cryopreservation facilities, ensuring safe, reliable, and sustainable operation for the protection of preserved individuals.

## Core Concepts

- **Facility Safety Standards**: Physical, technical, and operational safety requirements
- **Backup Power Systems**: Redundant power and cooling systems
- **Bankruptcy Protocol**: Asset transfer and continuation of care procedures
- **Global Recognition**: International certification and mutual recognition

## Certification Levels

```
LEVEL_1: Basic         - Meets minimum safety standards
LEVEL_2: Standard      - Enhanced redundancy and monitoring
LEVEL_3: Advanced      - Full automation and geographic backup
LEVEL_4: Excellence    - Gold standard with research capability
```
