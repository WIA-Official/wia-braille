The Legal Framework for the Space Age

As humanity ventures beyond Earth, new legal orders are needed. Who can own lunar resources? What laws apply to Mars settlements? How far can military activities extend in space?

This ebook comprehensively covers space law from the 1967 UN Outer Space Treaty to the 2020s Artemis Accords. International treaties, resource ownership, commercial regulations, space traffic management, military use, and future lunar/Mars governance are all systematically explained.
