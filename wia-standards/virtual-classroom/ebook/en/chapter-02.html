Video Streaming & Audio Setup

High-quality video and audio are essential for effective virtual classrooms. This chapter covers WebRTC technology, optimization techniques, and troubleshooting.

WebRTC Technology

Web Real-Time Communication (WebRTC) enables peer-to-peer audio and video streaming in browsers:

const config = {
  video: {
    codec: 'VP9',
    resolution: '1920x1080',
    frameRate: 30
  },
  audio: {
    codec: 'Opus',
    sampleRate: 48000
  }
};

Quality Optimization

  • Adaptive bitrate streaming
  • Bandwidth detection and adjustment
  • Network quality monitoring
  • Automatic resolution scaling

Audio Processing

Advanced audio features enhance clarity:

  • Noise cancellation and echo removal
  • Automatic gain control
  • Spatial audio for immersive experience
  • Individual volume controls
