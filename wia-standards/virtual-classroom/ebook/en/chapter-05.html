Attendance & Engagement

Track attendance and measure student engagement with advanced analytics and monitoring tools.

Attendance Tracking

  • Automatic check-in when joining
  • Facial recognition for verification
  • Late arrival and early departure tracking
  • Integration with student information systems

Engagement Metrics

const engagement = await classroom.getEngagementMetrics();
console.log(`Attention: ${engagement.averageAttention}%`);
console.log(`Participation: ${engagement.participationRate}%`);

Key Indicators

  • Attention level (eye tracking, screen focus)
  • Participation rate (questions, chat, polls)
  • Hand raises and verbal contributions
  • Quiz and poll responses
