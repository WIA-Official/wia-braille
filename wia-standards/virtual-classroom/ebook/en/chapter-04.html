Breakout Rooms & Group Work

Breakout rooms enable small group discussions and collaborative activities within larger virtual classrooms.

Creating Breakout Rooms

const rooms = await classroom.createBreakoutRooms({
  count: 5,
  duration: 15,
  autoAssign: true
});

Management Strategies

  • Automatic vs. manual assignment
  • Timer and countdown displays
  • Instructor roaming between rooms
  • Broadcast messages to all rooms
  • Activity monitoring dashboard

Effective Group Activities

  • Problem-solving exercises
  • Peer review sessions
  • Discussion groups
  • Collaborative projects
