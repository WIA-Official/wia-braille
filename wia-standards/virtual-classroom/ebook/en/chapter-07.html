Best Practices for Online Teaching

Effective online teaching requires different strategies than traditional classrooms. This chapter covers proven techniques for engaging virtual learners.

Engagement Techniques

  • Start with an icebreaker activity
  • Use polls every 10-15 minutes
  • Incorporate breakout discussions
  • Encourage camera use when appropriate
  • Vary presentation methods

Technical Setup

  • Good lighting (face the light source)
  • Quality microphone (USB or headset)
  • Neutral background or virtual background
  • Backup internet connection
  • Test setup before class

Managing Large Classes

  • Use waiting room to control entry
  • Mute all on entry
  • Assign teaching assistants as co-hosts
  • Monitor chat actively
  • Record sessions for later review
