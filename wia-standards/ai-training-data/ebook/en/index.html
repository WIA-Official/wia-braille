WIA-AI-007 Complete Implementation Guide

About This Guide

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for the WIA-AI-007 AI Training Data Standard. This standard provides a complete framework for managing, processing, and ensuring the quality of training data for artificial intelligence systems.

In accordance with the philosophy of 弘益人間 (Benefit All Humanity), this standard aims to promote ethical, high-quality, and privacy-preserving approaches to AI training data management.

This guide covers everything from foundational concepts to advanced implementation strategies, including dataset versioning, quality assurance, bias detection, and privacy-preserving techniques.

  • Chapter 8: Privacy-Preserving Training Data
