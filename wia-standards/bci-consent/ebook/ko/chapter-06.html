6.1 Initial Consent Workflow

The initial consent workflow is the most comprehensive, establishing the foundation for all future BCI interactions...

  1. Step 1: Capacity Assessment - Evaluate subject's ability to provide informed consent
  2. Step 2: Information Provision - Present risks, benefits, and alternatives
  3. Step 3: Understanding Verification - Confirm comprehension through teach-back or questionnaire
  4. Step 4: Voluntary Agreement - Ensure consent is freely given without coercion
  5. Step 5: Digital Signature - Capture cryptographic signature
  6. Step 6: Record Creation - Generate and store consent record
  7. Step 7: Confirmation - Provide copy to subject

6.2 Consent Modification Workflow

When users want to change their consent permissions, the modification workflow ensures they understand the implications...

  • Identify specific changes requested
  • Assess impact of modifications
  • Provide additional disclosure if needed
  • Verify understanding of changes
  • Capture new signature
  • Create new version with change tracking
  • Archive previous version

6.3 Consent Revocation Workflow

Users have the absolute right to revoke consent at any time. The revocation workflow ensures this right is honored while meeting legal obligations...

6.3.1 Revocation Steps

  1. Revocation Request: User initiates revocation through any available channel
  2. Identity Verification: Confirm requestor is authorized to revoke
  3. Confirmation: Ensure revocation is intentional and informed
  4. Immediate Cessation: Stop all data collection and processing immediately
  5. Data Disposition: Begin data deletion or anonymization process
  6. Notification: Inform all parties with access to data
  7. Documentation: Record revocation with timestamp and reason
  8. Confirmation to User: Provide written confirmation of revocation

6.3.2 Data Deletion Timeline

Data TypeDeletion TimelineExceptions
Real-time dataImmediateNone
Cached data24 hoursNone
Analytical data30 daysLegal hold
Backup data90 days (next backup cycle)Legal requirement
Anonymized aggregatesNot deleted (cannot re-identify)N/A

6.4 Consent Renewal Workflow

To ensure ongoing informed consent, periodic renewal is required...

6.4.1 Renewal Triggers

  • Approaching expiration date (30 days notice)
  • Significant protocol changes
  • New data use purposes
  • Regulatory requirement changes
  • Major device upgrades

6.4.2 Renewal Process

  1. Send renewal notification to user
  2. Highlight any changes since last consent
  3. Provide updated risk/benefit information
  4. Reassess capacity if indicated
  5. Obtain renewed consent signature
  6. Create new consent version linked to previous

6.5 Emergency Override Protocol

When immediate BCI intervention is required to prevent death or serious harm, emergency override procedures apply...

6.5.1 Conditions for Emergency Override

  • Immediate threat to life or risk of serious irreversible harm
  • Obtaining consent is impossible (unconscious, incapacitated)
  • Delay would significantly increase risk
  • Intervention is within standard of care

6.5.2 Authorization Requirements

  • Must be authorized by licensed physician
  • Detailed justification documented in real-time
  • Ethics board notified within 24 hours
  • Retrospective review required within 48 hours
  • Subject or family informed as soon as possible

6.5.3 Post-Override Requirements

  • Comprehensive incident report
  • Ethics board review of decision
  • Notification to regulatory authorities if required
  • Update to consent when subject regains capacity
  • Include in quality improvement review

6.6 Consent Withdrawal Rights

The protocol ensures subjects can exercise their right to withdraw consent easily and effectively...

6.6.1 Withdrawal Methods

  • Direct Request: Via web portal, mobile app, or phone
  • Written Notice: Email or physical letter
  • Verbal Request: To healthcare provider (must be documented)
  • Third-Party Request: Through legal representative
  • Automatic Withdrawal: Upon subject's death (unless post-mortem use specified)

6.6.2 Withdrawal Confirmation

Upon receiving withdrawal request:

  1. Verify identity of requester
  2. Confirm intentionality (not accidental)
  3. Provide information about consequences
  4. Process withdrawal immediately (no delay)
  5. Send written confirmation within 24 hours
  6. Complete data processing cessation within 48 hours

6.7 Partial Consent Modification

Users can modify specific permissions without revoking entire consent...

6.7.1 Granular Permission Changes

Scenario: User wants to stop AI training use but continue therapeutic use

Step 1: User selects permissions to modify
  - aiTraining: true → false
  - commercialUse: true → false
  - Other permissions unchanged

Step 2: System assesses impact
  - "Changing these permissions will affect:"
  - "- Your data will no longer be used to improve AI models"
  - "- Therapeutic benefits remain unchanged"
  - "- You can reverse this decision at any time"

Step 3: User confirms modification
  - Review changes
  - Sign modified consent
  - Effective immediately

Step 4: System updates permissions
  - New consent version created
  - Previous version archived
  - AI training systems notified to exclude subject
  - Change logged in audit trail

6.8 Special Population Workflows

6.8.1 Pediatric Consent Workflow

For subjects under age of majority (typically 18):

  1. Parental Consent: Legally authorized representative provides consent
  2. Child Assent: Child aged 7+ provides assent (agreement) when cognitively able
  3. Age-Appropriate Materials: Use materials suitable for child's development level
  4. Ongoing Re-Consent: As child matures, seek increasing participation in decisions
  5. Transition at Majority: When child reaches 18, transition to adult consent

6.8.2 Diminished Capacity Workflow

For subjects with cognitive impairment:

  1. Capacity Assessment: Formal evaluation using validated tools
  2. Supported Decision-Making: Involve support persons while respecting autonomy
  3. Simplified Materials: Use plain language, visual aids, videos
  4. Extended Time: Allow adequate time for understanding
  5. Iterative Consent: Break consent into smaller, manageable pieces
  6. Surrogate if Needed: Use legal representative if capacity insufficient
  7. Regular Reassessment: Capacity can fluctuate, reassess periodically

6.8.3 Emergency Consent Workflow

When immediate BCI intervention is medically necessary:

  1. Emergency Determination: Physician determines immediate threat to life/health
  2. Consent Impossible: Confirm subject cannot consent (unconscious, incapacitated)
  3. Legal Representative Unavailable: No authorized representative immediately available
  4. Medical Authorization: Physician authorizes emergency use
  5. Documentation: Complete emergency consent form with justification
  6. Time Limit: Emergency authorization valid 24-48 hours maximum
  7. Ethics Review: Submit for retrospective ethics review within 48 hours
  8. Transition: Obtain proper consent as soon as subject/representative available

6.9 Multi-Site Consent Coordination

For multi-center studies or distributed care...

6.9.1 Centralized Consent Repository

  • Central consent database accessible to all authorized sites
  • Real-time synchronization of consent status
  • Site-specific permissions and access controls
  • Audit trail includes site information

6.9.2 Consent Transfer Between Sites

When subject transfers care:

  1. Verify consent validity at original site
  2. Transfer consent record to new site securely
  3. New site reviews and accepts transferred consent
  4. Subject confirms consent remains valid
  5. Update consent record with new site information

6.12.2 Migration Between Versions

When protocol updates require new consent:

  1. Identify subjects with outdated consent versions

  3. Provide comparison showing what changed

  5. Maintain historical versions for audit
  6. Apply grandfathering provisions if appropriate
