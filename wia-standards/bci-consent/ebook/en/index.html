Cognitive liberty is a fundamental human right. The WIA BCI Consent Protocol ensures that brain-computer interface technology respects individual autonomy, protects neural privacy, and empowers users with meaningful control over their most intimate data.

About This Ebook

This official ebook provides a comprehensive guide to the WIA BCI Consent Protocol. It covers informed consent for brain-computer interfaces, neural data privacy protection, cognitive liberty rights, and international legal compliance across all regulatory frameworks.
