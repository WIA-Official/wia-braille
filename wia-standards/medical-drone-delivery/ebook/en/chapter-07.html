10% of medicines in low-income countries counterfeit ($200B). Security prevents theft, counterfeiting, tampering.

Tamper-Proof

Reinforced shell, electronic locks, one-way seals, sensors, alarms.

Encryption

AES-256, TLS 1.3, RSA-2048. End-to-end protection.

Biometric Auth

Fingerprint (<0.001% FAR), facial recognition (>99.9%), multi-factor (RFID+bio+PIN+SMS).

GPS Tracking

Real-time ±2m (RTK ±2cm). Geofencing alerts. Anti-jamming multi-GNSS.

Blockchain

Immutable supply chain record. Smart contracts auto-verify. Counterfeit prevention via QR codes.
