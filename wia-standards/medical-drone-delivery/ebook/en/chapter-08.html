Standardized landing pads and EHR integration enable seamless operations.

Landing Pad Standards

5m×5m minimum, white 'H' marking, LED lights, within 50m of pharmacy/ER.

Precision Landing

RTK GPS ±5cm accuracy. Vision-based 'H' recognition. LiDAR altitude measurement.

HL7 FHIR Integration

MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense, Task, Device, Location resources enable auto-ordering, tracking, inventory management.

Automated Workflow

Prescription→inventory check→auto-request→approval→dispatch→tracking→receipt→inventory update.

AI Inventory

Demand forecasting, RFID tracking, FEFO principle, auto-reordering.

KPIs

On-time >95%, delivery <30min, temp >99%, landing >99.5%, availability >99.9%.

Future

Autonomous charging, swarm drones, AI medical assistant, UAM integration.

Conclusion: Medical drone delivery transforms healthcare with fast, reliable, cost-effective delivery, saving countless lives worldwide.
