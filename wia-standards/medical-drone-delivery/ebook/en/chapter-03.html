Vaccines are critical for public health, yet traditional cold chain systems fail 50% of the time globally, resulting in $34B annual waste. Drones solve this through direct last-mile delivery.

Vaccine Temperature Requirements

  • Most vaccines: 2-8°C
  • COVID-19 (Pfizer): -70°C
  • COVID-19 (Moderna): -20°C
  • Polio OPV: -20°C storage, 2-8°C transport

Rwanda Zipline Program

Operating since 2016, Zipline delivers vaccines to 2,000+ health facilities across Rwanda using fixed-wing drones with 80km range. By 2021, over 700,000 deliveries completed, with vaccination rates increasing 30%.

Ghana Network

Four distribution centers cover 80% of Ghana's population (24M people). Drones deliver routine childhood vaccines (DTP, MMR, polio, BCG) plus COVID-19 vaccines, reducing stock-outs by 80%.

Container Design

Multi-layer insulation with VIP, PCM, and active Peltier cooling maintains 2-8°C for 2-4 hours passively, longer with active systems. IoT sensors monitor temperature every 10 seconds with cloud upload.

Last-Mile Solution

Drones bypass the most difficult and expensive part of vaccine delivery - the final leg from regional hub to remote clinic. This eliminates multiple handling steps where cold chain typically breaks.

Disaster Response

During earthquakes, floods, or outbreaks, drones deliver vaccines when roads are impassable. COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated value with contactless delivery to isolated areas.

AI Demand Forecasting

Machine learning predicts vaccine demand based on historical data, seasonal patterns, population demographics, and outbreak models, enabling proactive distribution.
