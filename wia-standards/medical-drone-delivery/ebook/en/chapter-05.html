50% of vaccines globally wasted due to cold chain failure ($34B). Drones minimize complexity and improve reliability.

Cooling Systems

Passive: VIP, PCM (2-4hr). Active: Peltier (±0.5°C), Compressor (COP 2-3), Stirling (-80°C).

Monitoring

Multi-sensor logging every 10sec. Real-time alerts at 3 levels. Blockchain immutable records.

Compliance

WHO PQS, GDP, USP <1079>, ISO 13485.
