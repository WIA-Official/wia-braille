Chapter 08

Future Space Sustainability

8.1 Mega-Constellations

Starlink (42,000 planned, 5,000+ deployed), OneWeb (6,372), Kuiper (3,236), Guowang (12,992). 100,000+ active satellites by 2030.

8.2 Space Traffic Management

Need ATC-like system: real-time tracking, data sharing, Right of Way rules, automated coordination. Governance model TBD (UN? Consortium? Public-private?).

8.3 Key Technologies

AI autonomous avoidance, electric propulsion, on-orbit servicing (fuel, repair, relocate), space-based sensors.

8.4 Sustainable Business

ADR market growth, circular economy (recycle/reuse), orbit usage fees, insurance requirements, ESG ratings.

8.5 Scenarios

Optimistic: 5-year rule global, 50-100 removals/year, clean orbits. Pessimistic: Kessler syndrome, 800-1000km unusable. Realistic: managed growth with continuous effort.

💡 Key Takeaways

This chapter covered essential aspects of future space sustainability. Understanding these concepts is crucial for effective space debris management and ensuring long-term sustainability of space activities.
