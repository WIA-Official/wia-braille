Chapter 04

Collision Avoidance

4.1 Conjunction Analysis

Daily screening of all catalog objects. TCA (Time of Closest Approach) and Miss Distance calculated. Probability of Collision (Pc) computed using covariance matrices.

4.2 Decision Thresholds

Pc > 1/10,000 triggers detailed analysis. Pc > 1/1,000 typically requires maneuver. Decisions made 24 hours before TCA.

4.3 Maneuver Strategies

Radial boost/lower (1-5 m/s) most efficient. Along-track (5-20 m/s) for timing adjustments. ΔV budget management critical for mission life.

4.4 Automation

SpaceX Starlink uses autonomous avoidance. AI/ML optimizes maneuver planning. Human-in-loop for high-value assets.

4.5 Coordination

Space Data Association facilitates operator coordination. No formal 'Right of Way' rules yet. Need for Space Traffic Management (STM) system.

💡 Key Takeaways

This chapter covered essential aspects of collision avoidance. Understanding these concepts is crucial for effective space debris management and ensuring long-term sustainability of space activities.
