Chapter 06

Mitigation Guidelines

6.1 International Standards

UN COPUOS 7 guidelines (2007). IADC requirements. ISO 24113:2019 technical standard.

6.2 25-Year Rule

LEO satellites must deorbit within 25 years post-mission. FCC proposed 5-year rule (2024). Compliance ~60% currently.

6.3 Passivation

Drain all propellants, vent pressurized gases, discharge batteries, stop flywheels. Prevents explosions (Delta 2nd stages, Briz-M failures).

6.4 Design for Demise

Use low-melting materials (aluminum), minimize titanium/stainless steel. Ensure complete burn-up on re-entry. OneWeb implements D4D.

6.5 GEO Disposal

Raise to graveyard orbit: GEO +235km minimum, typically +300km. Accounts for solar radiation pressure over centuries.

💡 Key Takeaways

This chapter covered essential aspects of mitigation guidelines. Understanding these concepts is crucial for effective space debris management and ensuring long-term sustainability of space activities.
