Chapter 02

Debris Tracking Systems

2.1 Space Surveillance Networks

US SSN tracks ~34,000 objects using 30+ radars and optical sensors globally. Space Fence (2019) can track 5cm objects in LEO.

2.2 Radar Systems

Phased array radars provide all-weather tracking. AN/FPS-85 can detect 10cm LEO objects. Fence mode for detection, track mode for precise orbits.

2.3 Optical Tracking

GEODSS telescopes track GEO objects. Can detect basketball-sized objects at 36,000km. Limited by weather and daylight. SLR provides mm-precision.

2.4 Data Integration

TLE (Two-Line Element) format distributes orbital data. Space-Track.org provides free access. CDM (Conjunction Data Message) warns of close approaches.

2.5 International Networks

EU SST, Russian SKKP, Chinese SSA systems. Commercial services: LeoLabs (2cm tracking goal), ExoAnalytic, AGI ComSpOC.

💡 Key Takeaways

This chapter covered essential aspects of debris tracking systems. Understanding these concepts is crucial for effective space debris management and ensuring long-term sustainability of space activities.
