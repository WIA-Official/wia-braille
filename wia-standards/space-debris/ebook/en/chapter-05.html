Chapter 05

Active Debris Removal

5.1 ADR Necessity

IADC recommends removing 5-10 large objects annually. Even zero launches would see 30% debris increase without ADR.

5.2 Capture Methods

Robotic arms (ClearSpace-1), nets (RemoveDEBRIS), harpoons, magnetic capture. Each suited for different target types.

5.3 Deorbit Techniques

Direct re-entry, drag sails (increase atmospheric drag), electrodynamic tethers (use Earth's magnetic field), GEO graveyard orbits (+300km).

5.4 Demonstrations

RemoveDEBRIS (2018): net, harpoon tests. ELSA-d (2021): magnetic docking. ClearSpace-1 (2026): first commercial ADR mission.

5.5 Economics

Single removal: $50-150M currently. Multi-target missions more cost-effective. Market projected $500M-1B by 2030.

💡 Key Takeaways

This chapter covered essential aspects of active debris removal. Understanding these concepts is crucial for effective space debris management and ensuring long-term sustainability of space activities.
