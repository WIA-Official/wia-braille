Chapter 07

International Cooperation & Regulation

7.1 Space Treaties

Outer Space Treaty (1967): launching state retains jurisdiction. Liability Convention (1972): damage compensation. Registration Convention (1976).

7.2 UN COPUOS

95 member states. Develops debris mitigation guidelines (soft law). Scientific & Technical, and Legal Subcommittees.

7.3 IADC

13 space agencies coordinate. Technical guidelines foundation for COPUOS. Joint re-entry campaigns. Environment models (ORDEM, MASTER).

7.4 National Regulations

US FCC: debris assessment required. EU: Space Surveillance & Tracking. France, Germany, UK, Japan have space laws. Harmonization needed.

7.5 Legal Gaps

Defunct debris still 'owned', ADR permission unclear. No enforcement mechanism. Need binding international treaty.

💡 Key Takeaways

This chapter covered essential aspects of international cooperation & regulation. Understanding these concepts is crucial for effective space debris management and ensuring long-term sustainability of space activities.
