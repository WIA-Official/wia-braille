About This Guide

Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) is revolutionizing healthcare delivery. It enables patients to receive continuous care outside hospital settings, achieving better health outcomes, reducing costs, and improving quality of life.

This comprehensive guide provides practical guidance on designing, implementing, and operating RPM systems. It's essential reading for healthcare providers, technology developers, policy makers, and anyone seeking to understand remote monitoring solutions.

Following the philosophy of 弘益人間 (Benefit All Humanity), we're building a future where all patients can access high-quality health monitoring services regardless of location or economic status.

Learning Objectives

Upon completing this guide, you will be able to:

  • Understand core components of RPM systems
  • Select appropriate monitoring technologies and wearables
  • Design and implement effective alert and notification systems
  • Develop customized monitoring protocols for chronic diseases
  • Implement data analytics and AI for predictive healthcare
  • Integrate clinical workflows and navigate insurance billing
  • Develop strategies to increase patient engagement and adherence
  • Meet regulatory requirements including HIPAA and GDPR
