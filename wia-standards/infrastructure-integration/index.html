Key Projects & Use Cases

🚄 Trans-Korean Railway (TKR)

Connecting Seoul to Sinuiju via Kaesong and Pyongyang, integrating with Trans-Siberian Railway for continental access. Includes standardized signaling, communication systems, and unified ticketing infrastructure.

🛣️ Gyeongui Highway Reconnection

Restoration and modernization of the Gyeongui highway connecting Seoul-Kaesong-Sinuiju with intelligent transportation systems, automated toll collection, and unified traffic management.

⚡ Unified Power Grid

Integration of electrical power systems across the peninsula enabling renewable energy sharing, load balancing, and emergency power distribution during natural disasters.

💧 Han River Basin Management

Coordinated water resource management including flood control, hydroelectric power generation, irrigation systems, and environmental protection across shared watersheds.

🏗️ Kaesong Industrial Complex

Infrastructure standards for joint industrial zones including utilities, telecommunications, transportation access, and environmental monitoring systems.

🌉 DMZ Peace Bridge Initiative

Design and construction standards for symbolic and functional bridges across the DMZ, integrating pedestrian, vehicle, and rail traffic with tourism infrastructure.
