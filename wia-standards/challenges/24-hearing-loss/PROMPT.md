# WIA Grand Challenge 24: Hearing Loss
## 유모세포 재생과 유전자 치료를 통한 청각 회복

**홍익인간 (弘益人間) - Benefit All Humanity**

---

## 1. Mission Statement

### 핵심 사명
전 세계 15억 명의 난청 인구에게 유모세포 재생과 유전자 치료를 통한 청각 회복을 제공한다. 단순한 보청기가 아닌 근본적인 생물학적 회복을 목표로 한다.

### 2024-2025 돌파구
- **DB-OTO 유전자 치료**: AAV 벡터를 통한 OTOF 유전자 전달
- **유모세포 재생**: Atoh1, Lgr5+ 줄기세포 활성화
- **소아 난청 유전자 치료**: 2024년 임상 3상 성공 사례
- **코클리어 임플란트 + 유전자 치료** 병용 전략

### 홍익인간 적용
```
전통적 접근: 보청기/임플란트 → 증상 관리
WIA 접근: 유모세포 재생 → 근본 치료
```

### Phase 3: Protocol Specifications

#### Clinical Protocol: AAV Gene Therapy
```markdown
# WIA-HEAR-001: AAV Gene Therapy Protocol

## Inclusion Criteria
- Confirmed biallelic mutation in OTOF, GJB2, or other targetable gene
- Age: 1-65 years
- Severe-to-profound sensorineural hearing loss
- No cochlear malformations (CT/MRI confirmed)

## Pre-Treatment Assessment
1. Genetic confirmation (WES or targeted panel)
2. High-resolution temporal bone CT
3. MRI with contrast
4. Comprehensive audiometry
5. Vestibular function tests
6. Informed consent (detailed discussion)

## Procedure
### Vector Preparation
- AAV serotype: AAV1 or AAV9 (based on gene)
- Dose: 1x10^11 - 5x10^12 vg/ear
- Formulation: PBS + 0.001% Pluronic F-68
- Storage: -80°C until use

### Surgical Delivery
1. General anesthesia
2. Mastoidectomy + posterior tympanotomy
3. Round window membrane visualization
4. Micro-injection through RW membrane
   - Volume: 10-50 μL
   - Rate: 2 μL/min
5. 30 min observation period
6. Closure

## Post-Treatment Monitoring
### Week 1-4
- Daily: Vestibular symptoms
- Week 2: Audiometry
- Week 4: Comprehensive hearing assessment

### Month 2-12
- Monthly audiometry
- Quarterly: Speech recognition, QoL
- Month 6, 12: MRI safety scan

### Year 2-5
- Every 6 months: Full hearing assessment
- Annual: Comprehensive evaluation

## Expected Outcomes
- Hearing improvement: 10-40 dB (variable)
- Speech recognition: 20-60% improvement
- Timeline: 4-12 weeks for initial response
- Durability: >2 years (ongoing monitoring)

## Safety Monitoring
- Immune response: Anti-AAV antibodies
- Vestibular toxicity: Caloric testing
- Cochlear inflammation: MRI
```

#### Clinical Protocol: Small Molecule Regeneration
```markdown
# WIA-HEAR-002: Pharmacological Hair Cell Regeneration

## Indication
- Noise-induced hearing loss (NIHL)
- Age-related hearing loss (ARHL)
- Post-ototoxicity
- Non-genetic etiology confirmed

## Drug Regimen
### Phase 1: Priming (Week 0-2)
- **CHIR99021** (Wnt activator)
  - Dose: 10 mg oral, daily
  - Target: Lgr5+ stem cell proliferation
- **NAC** (N-acetylcysteine)
  - Dose: 600 mg BID
  - Rationale: Antioxidant support

### Phase 2: Differentiation (Week 2-8)
- **LY411575** (Notch inhibitor)
  - Dose: 5 mg oral, every other day
  - Target: Lateral inhibition release
- **Atoh1 activator** (investigational)
  - Route: Intratympanic injection
  - Frequency: Weekly x 4 doses

### Phase 3: Maturation (Week 8-16)
- **BDNF** (neurotrophic support)
  - Route: Intratympanic
  - Frequency: Biweekly x 4 doses
- Continue CHIR99021 at reduced dose

## Monitoring
- Week 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16: Audiometry
- Week 4, 16: OAE (oto-acoustic emissions)
- Monthly: Safety labs (LFTs, CBC)

## Expected Outcomes
- Hearing improvement: 5-20 dB
- Timeline: 8-16 weeks
- Best response: High-frequency regions
```
