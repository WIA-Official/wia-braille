WIA-MED-020

Mental Health Monitoring Standards

弘益人間 · Benefit All Humanity

Comprehensive standards for digital mental health monitoring systems, covering digital phenotyping, mood tracking, crisis intervention, and privacy-preserving mental health technologies that empower individuals while maintaining the highest ethical standards.

🎯 Core Features

📱

Digital Phenotyping

Passive monitoring of smartphone usage patterns, social interactions, and activity levels to detect mental health changes early.

📊

Mood Tracking

Evidence-based mood logging systems with validated questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7) and real-time trend analysis.

🔍

Screening Tools

Standardized anxiety, depression, and PTSD screening instruments with clinical validation and cultural adaptation.

💭

CBT Integration

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy apps with guided exercises, thought records, and behavioral activation tracking.

🚨

Crisis Intervention

Automated risk detection, immediate crisis resources, and emergency contact protocols for suicide prevention.

👨‍⚕️

Therapist Connection

Secure integration with mental health professionals, progress sharing, and collaborative care coordination.

🎤

Voice & Text Analysis

AI-powered sentiment analysis of voice patterns and written communication to assess emotional state.

🔒

Privacy Protection

End-to-end encryption, data minimization, and strict compliance with mental health privacy regulations.
