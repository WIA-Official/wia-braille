About This eBook

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on AI Governance under the WIA-AI-020 standard. This eBook provides practical frameworks, strategies, and best practices for implementing responsible AI governance in your organization.

Through eight detailed chapters, you'll learn how to build robust governance structures, manage AI risks, ensure regulatory compliance, and foster ethical AI development that benefits all humanity.

What You'll Learn:

  • Foundational principles of AI governance
  • Framework design and implementation strategies
  • Risk management and mitigation techniques
  • Ethics integration and stakeholder engagement
  • Regulatory compliance and audit processes
  • Organizational structures for AI oversight
  • Practical implementation roadmaps
