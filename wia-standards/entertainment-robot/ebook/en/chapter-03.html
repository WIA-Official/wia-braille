Chapter 3: WIA-EDU-025 Standard Overview

3.1 Core Architecture

The WIA-EDU-025 standard defines four core pillars for entertainment robots:

  1. Storytelling & Narrative Systems: Standardized formats for interactive stories, character development, and choice mechanics
  2. Performance & Expression: Choreography protocols, emotional display systems, and audience interaction standards
  3. Emotional Intelligence: Emotion recognition, empathetic response generation, and therapeutic protocol support
  4. Play-Based Learning: Game mechanics, educational objective integration, and outcome measurement

3.2 Design Principles

1. Engagement-First

Educational value must never compromise entertainment value. The standard supports genuinely engaging experiences that happen to deliver learning outcomes.

2. Safety & Privacy by Design

All protocols include child safety, emotional well-being protection, and privacy compliance (COPPA, GDPR, HIPAA for therapeutic applications).

3. Creative Freedom

The standard provides structure without limiting creativity. Content creators maintain artistic freedom while ensuring compatibility.

4. Measurable Impact

Educational outcomes, emotional well-being improvements, and therapeutic progress must be measurable and verifiable.

Key Innovation: The standard separates content (stories, performances, games) from delivery (robot platform), enabling a rich content ecosystem while maintaining platform competition on quality and price.

3.3 Technical Stack

  • Data Format: JSON-LD for semantic interoperability
  • Content Schema: Extensible schemas for stories, performances, games, and therapeutic sessions
  • API Protocol: RESTful APIs with WebSocket support for real-time interaction
  • Credential System: W3C Verifiable Credentials for achievements and progress
  • Privacy Layer: Zero-knowledge proof support and consent management
