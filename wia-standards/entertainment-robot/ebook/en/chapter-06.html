Chapter 6: Emotional Intelligence & Therapeutic Applications

6.1 Emotion Recognition

The standard defines multi-modal emotion detection combining facial expression analysis, voice tone recognition, body language interpretation, and conversational sentiment analysis. Emotion models are standardized to enable consistent recognition across platforms.

Privacy First: All emotion data is processed locally when possible. Cloud processing requires explicit consent and follows strict privacy protocols (HIPAA for therapeutic contexts).

6.2 Empathetic Response Generation

Detected emotions trigger appropriate empathetic responses:

  • Validation: Acknowledging and normalizing emotions
  • Comfort: Soothing responses for distress
  • Encouragement: Motivation for frustration or challenge
  • Celebration: Amplifying positive emotions
  • Redirection: Gentle shifting of overwhelming emotions

6.3 Therapeutic Protocols

For therapeutic applications (autism support, anxiety management, social skills development), the standard defines evidence-based protocols validated by clinical research. These include:

  • Structured session formats with warmup, activity, and cooldown
  • Progress tracking aligned with therapeutic goals
  • Safety protocols and emergency response procedures
  • Professional supervision integration
  • Parent/caregiver communication systems

6.4 Ethical Considerations

The standard includes strict ethical guidelines:

  • Robots must never replace human therapeutic relationships
  • All therapeutic use requires professional oversight
  • Emotional data must be protected with highest security
  • Children's emotional well-being takes precedence over engagement metrics
