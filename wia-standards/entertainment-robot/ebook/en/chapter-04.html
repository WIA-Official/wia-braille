Chapter 4: Interactive Storytelling Systems

4.1 Story Data Format

The interactive story format supports branching narratives, character development, emotional arcs, and educational objective integration. Stories are defined in JSON-LD with semantic metadata enabling cross-platform compatibility.

Example Story Structure: A story includes metadata (title, genre, age range), chapters with narrative beats, character profiles with emotional states, choice points that branch the narrative, and embedded learning objectives tied to story outcomes.

4.2 Choice Mechanics

The standard defines several choice types:

  • Narrative Choices: Affect story direction and character relationships
  • Educational Choices: Test comprehension or critical thinking
  • Emotional Choices: Develop empathy and emotional intelligence
  • Creative Choices: Allow open-ended input (drawings, words, melodies)

4.3 Character System

Characters are defined with personality traits, emotional ranges, relationship dynamics, and learning objectives they embody. The robot can portray multiple characters with distinct voices and mannerisms.

4.4 Educational Integration

Every story includes explicit learning objectives aligned with educational standards (Common Core, NGSS, SEL frameworks). Progress tracking enables parents and educators to see what skills are being developed through each story.
