Chapter 5: Performance & Theater Robots

5.1 Performance Programming

Performance robots execute choreographed shows combining movement, speech, music, lighting, and special effects. The standard defines a timeline-based programming model where performances are composed of acts, scenes, and beats.

5.2 Audience Interaction

Unlike passive entertainment, educational performance robots incorporate audience participation:

  • Call-and-response segments that reinforce learning
  • Volunteer participation for demonstrations
  • Real-time polls affecting show direction
  • Q&A sessions integrated into performance flow
Educational Theater: The standard enables performances that teach science through magic shows, history through time-travel adventures, and social-emotional learning through character-driven dramas.

5.3 Multi-Robot Coordination

The protocol supports synchronized performances with multiple robots, enabling ensemble casts, complex choreography, and richer storytelling. Robots communicate via the standard's real-time coordination protocol.

5.4 Performance Assessment

Educational objectives are measured through embedded assessments:

  • Pre-show baseline checks
  • Participation tracking during performance
  • Post-show comprehension questions
  • Long-term retention follow-ups
