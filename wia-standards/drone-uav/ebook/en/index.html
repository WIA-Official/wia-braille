Drone (UAV)

WIA-SPACE-017 English Ebook

A Drone, or Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), is an aircraft that operates without a human pilot onboard. This comprehensive ebook covers everything from basic principles to advanced autonomous flight technologies, and explores diverse industrial applications.

From hobby drones to commercial UAVs, military systems to delivery drones—learn about all aspects of drone technology in this systematic guide.

📚 Table of Contents

  • CHAPTER 01 Drone Overview History and evolution, classification systems (multicopter, fixed-wing, VTOL), major applications
  • CHAPTER 02 Drone Hardware Frame structures, motors and ESC, propellers, battery systems, hardware design principles
  • CHAPTER 03 Sensors and Payloads Cameras and gimbals, LiDAR, thermal imaging, multispectral sensors, payload integration
  • CHAPTER 04 Flight Control Flight Controller (FC), GPS and positioning, IMU and attitude control, PID control, stabilization
  • CHAPTER 05 Communication Systems RF communication, telemetry, MAVLink protocol, FPV video transmission, remote control systems
  • CHAPTER 06 Autonomous Flight Waypoint navigation, SLAM technology, obstacle avoidance, autonomous landing, AI-based flight planning
  • CHAPTER 07 Regulations and Certification FAA Part 107, EASA regulations, safety standards, flight authorization procedures
  • CHAPTER 08 Industrial Applications Delivery and logistics, precision agriculture, aerial photography, infrastructure inspection, disaster response
