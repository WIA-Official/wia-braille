Chapter 05

Interaction and Accessibility

♿ Universal Design in XR

XR education must be accessible to all students, regardless of physical, cognitive, or sensory abilities.

Interaction Methods

  • Hand Tracking: Natural gesture-based interaction without controllers
  • Voice Commands: Hands-free navigation for accessibility
  • Eye Tracking: Gaze-based selection for motor impairments
  • Controllers: Precise manipulation with haptic feedback

Accessibility Features

  • Colorblind modes for visual impairments
  • Subtitles and sign language interpretation
  • Seated mode for wheelchair users
  • Reduced motion for comfort and safety
  • Adjustable text sizes and contrast
弘益人間: Accessibility in XR ensures that all students, regardless of ability, can benefit from immersive education.
