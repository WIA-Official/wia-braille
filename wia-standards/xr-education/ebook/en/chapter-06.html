XR Learning Analytics

📊 Measuring Learning in 3D

XR environments provide rich data about student engagement, interaction patterns, and learning progress that traditional classrooms cannot capture.

XR-Specific Metrics

  • Gaze Tracking: Heatmaps showing where students focus attention
  • Spatial Navigation: Paths taken through virtual environments
  • Object Interaction: Which elements students engage with
  • Gesture Analysis: How students manipulate 3D objects
  • Collaboration Patterns: Social dynamics in multiplayer sessions

Privacy and Ethics

Student data must be handled with care, following FERPA, COPPA, and GDPR guidelines. Implement data minimization and obtain proper consent for biometric data collection.
