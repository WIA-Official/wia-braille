3D Content Creation

🎨 Building Educational XR Experiences

Creating compelling XR content requires understanding of 3D modeling, game engines, and educational design principles.

Development Platforms

  • Unity: Industry-standard for XR development with extensive asset store
  • Unreal Engine: High-fidelity graphics for photorealistic experiences
  • WebXR: Browser-based XR for universal accessibility
  • A-Frame: Web framework for quick AR/VR prototyping

Content Optimization

Performance is critical in XR. Optimize polygon counts, texture sizes, and lighting to maintain smooth frame rates across devices.
