Chapter 1: Introduction to AI Diagnosis

"AI has the potential to revolutionize medical diagnosis—detecting diseases earlier, reducing errors, and democratizing access to expert-level healthcare. WIA-MED-009 ensures this potential is realized safely."

1.1 The Vision of Medical AI

AI Diagnosis leverages machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing to analyze medical data and diagnose diseases.

🎯 Core Objectives

  • Early Detection — Identify subtle patterns humans might miss
  • Improved Accuracy — Reduce misdiagnosis rates
  • Accessibility — Bring expert-level diagnosis to underserved areas
  • Efficiency — Reduce diagnosis time and physician burden
  • Personalization — Tailored diagnosis and treatment

1.2 Key Application Areas

🩺 Medical Imaging

AI analyzes X-rays, CT, MRI, ultrasound for disease detection. Examples: pneumonia detection, breast cancer screening, diabetic retinopathy.

💊 Clinical Decision Support

AI analyzes electronic health records to suggest diagnoses, flag drug interactions, and recommend treatments.

🧬 Genomics & Precision Medicine

AI analyzes genetic data to identify hereditary diseases, predict drug responses, and guide targeted therapy.

1.3 Why Standards Matter

Without rigorous standards, AI diagnosis systems risk:

  • ❌ Inaccurate diagnoses harming patients
  • ❌ Bias against certain demographics
  • ❌ Unexplainable "black box" decisions
  • ❌ Security vulnerabilities exposing patient data
  • ❌ Regulatory rejection and deployment delays

WIA-MED-009 solves these challenges with comprehensive requirements for safety, fairness, transparency, and clinical validation.

1.4 The WIA Philosophy: 弘益人間

弘益人間

"Benefit All Humanity" — An ancient Korean principle

Every decision in WIA-MED-009 asks: "Does this benefit humanity?"

This standard ensures AI diagnosis is:

  • ✓ Safe and thoroughly validated before deployment
  • ✓ Transparent and explainable to physicians and patients
