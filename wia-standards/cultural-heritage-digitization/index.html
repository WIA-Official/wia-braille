Key Features

📸

3D Scanning

High-resolution photogrammetry and LiDAR scanning for accurate digital replicas of artifacts and monuments

💾

Digital Preservation

Long-term archival standards ensuring cultural heritage remains accessible for future generations

🌐

Virtual Heritage Tours

Immersive VR/AR experiences allowing global access to historical sites and museums

🔍

Historical Reconstruction

AI-powered reconstruction of damaged or lost cultural artifacts based on historical data

📊

Metadata Standards

Rich Dublin Core and CIDOC-CRM compliant metadata for comprehensive cultural documentation

♿

Cultural Accessibility

WCAG-compliant interfaces making cultural heritage accessible to all, regardless of physical limitations
