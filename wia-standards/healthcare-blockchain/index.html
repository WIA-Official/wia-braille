WIA-MED-024

Healthcare Blockchain Standards

弘益人間 · Benefit All Humanity

Comprehensive standards for blockchain technology in healthcare, covering medical record immutability, patient consent chains, drug supply tracking, clinical trial integrity, and decentralized health data systems that empower patients while ensuring privacy, security, and interoperability.

🎯 Core Features

🔒

Medical Record Immutability

Tamper-proof medical records with cryptographic verification, ensuring data integrity and complete audit trails for all modifications.

✍️

Patient Consent Chains

Blockchain-based consent management with granular permissions, time-bound access, and transparent revocation mechanisms.

💊

Drug Supply Chain Tracking

End-to-end pharmaceutical traceability from manufacturer to patient, preventing counterfeit drugs and ensuring authenticity.

🧪

Clinical Trial Data Integrity

Immutable recording of trial protocols, patient enrollment, data collection, and results to prevent fraud and enhance trust.

🔄

Health Data Sharing

Secure, patient-controlled data exchange across healthcare providers with cryptographic access control and audit logs.

📋

Smart Contracts for Insurance

Automated claims processing, pre-authorization workflows, and transparent policy execution with reduced administrative costs.

🆔

Decentralized Identity

Self-sovereign patient identity management with verifiable credentials, privacy preservation, and cross-border portability.

🔗

Interoperability Standards

Cross-chain communication, FHIR integration, and standardized data formats for seamless healthcare ecosystem connectivity.
