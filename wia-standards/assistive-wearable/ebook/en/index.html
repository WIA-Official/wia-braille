"Benefit All Humanity"

Independence and safety are fundamental human rights. The WIA Assistive Wearable Standard ensures that smart wearable technology empowers individuals with disabilities, elderly users, and anyone needing assistive support to live independently and safely.

About This Ebook

This official ebook provides a comprehensive guide to the WIA Assistive Wearable Standard. It covers the complete specification across all four phases, from device data formats to ecosystem integration with mobile platforms and health services.
