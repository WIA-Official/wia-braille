# PET-GENOME

## Pet Genome Standard

> "Hongik Ingan" - Benefit all humanity
>
> Understanding our companions at the deepest level for their health and happiness.

## Overview

PET-GENOME establishes standards for pet genetic data management, disease prediction, personalized care, and identity verification including cloning scenarios.

## Key Components

### Genetic Testing
- DNA sequencing standards
- Breed identification
- Genetic marker analysis
- Disease risk assessment

### Identity Verification
- Genetic fingerprinting
- Clone identity continuity
- Parentage verification
- Individual identification

### Personalized Care
- Genetic-based nutrition recommendations
- Drug sensitivity screening
- Preventive care protocols
- Longevity optimization

### Data Management
- Genetic data storage standards
- Privacy and consent
- Cross-platform interoperability
- Long-term preservation

## Supported Species

- Dogs (Canis familiaris)
- Cats (Felis catus)
- Horses (Equus caballus)
- Birds (various species)
- Exotic pets (rabbits, ferrets, reptiles)

## Related Standards

- PET-HEALTH-PASSPORT - Health records
- PET-EMOTION - Emotional interface
- PET-LEGACY - Legacy management
- PET-CARE-ROBOT - Care automation
- PET-WELFARE-GLOBAL - Welfare certification
