2.1 Calorie Calculation Methods

Atwater System (Standard):

  • Carbohydrates: 4 kcal/g
  • Protein: 4 kcal/g
  • Fat: 9 kcal/g
  • Alcohol: 7 kcal/g

Example: Chicken Breast 100g

Protein 31g × 4 = 124 kcal
Fat 3.6g × 9 = 32.4 kcal
Carbs 0g × 4 = 0 kcal
─────────────────────
Total = 156.4 kcal

2.2 Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) Measurement

Mifflin-St Jeor Formula (Most Accurate for Modern Population):

  • Men: (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) - (5 × age) + 5
  • Women: (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) - (5 × age) - 161

Example: 30-year-old Male, 75kg, 175cm

BMR = (10 × 75) + (6.25 × 175) - (5 × 30) + 5
    = 750 + 1093.75 - 150 + 5 = 1,698.75 kcal

TDEE (Moderately Active): 1,699 × 1.55 = 2,633 kcal

  • General Health: 0.8g/kg body weight
  • Strength Training (Muscle Gain): 1.6-2.2g/kg
  • Endurance Athletes: 1.2-1.4g/kg
  • Elderly (Prevent Loss): 1.0-1.2g/kg

Fats Classification

  • Saturated Fat: Limit to <10% of calories
  • Monounsaturated (MUFA): Olive oil, avocado - Heart healthy
  • Polyunsaturated (PUFA): Omega-3 (fish) & Omega-6 (vegetable oils) - Anti-inflammatory
  • Trans Fats: Avoid completely - Increases heart disease risk

Omega-3 Goals:

  • General Health: 250-500mg EPA+DHA/day
  • Cardiovascular Prevention: 1,000mg/day
  • Inflammatory Conditions: 2,000-4,000mg/day

2.5 Hydration Monitoring

Daily Water Intake:

  • Adult Men: 3.7 L/day (3.0L beverages + 0.7L food)
  • Adult Women: 2.7 L/day (2.2L beverages + 0.5L food)

Activity Adjustments:

  • Moderate Exercise (30 min): +350-500 mL
  • Intense Exercise (1 hour): +1,000-1,500 mL
  • Hot Weather: +500-1,000 mL

Dehydration Signs:

  • Mild (1-2%): Thirst, slight fatigue
  • Moderate (3-5%): Headache, dark urine, concentration problems
  • Severe (>5%): Dizziness, rapid heartbeat, confusion - Seek medical help

  • □ Trans fats minimized
  • □ Adequate hydration
