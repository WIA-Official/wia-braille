1.1 Importance of Nutrition Tracking

Nutrition tracking is essential for health management, disease prevention, and goal achievement. Research shows that people who track their nutrition are twice as likely to achieve their health goals.

Benefits:

  • Disease prevention and management (diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular disease)
  • Weight control and obesity prevention
  • Immune system strengthening
  • Energy level optimization
  • Mental health improvement

1.3 Nutrient Classification System

Macronutrients

Carbohydrates (4 kcal/g)

  • Simple: Glucose, fructose, sucrose - Fast energy, blood sugar spike
  • Complex: Starches, fiber - Slow digestion, stable blood sugar
  • Recommended: 45-65% of total calories

Protein (4 kcal/g)

  • Complete: Animal sources - All 9 essential amino acids
  • Incomplete: Plant sources - Combine for complete profile
  • Recommended: 10-35% of total calories (1.6-2.2g/kg for strength training)

Fat (9 kcal/g)

  • Saturated: Limit to <10% of total calories
  • Unsaturated: Heart-healthy (olive oil, avocado, fish)
  • Trans fats: Avoid completely
  • Recommended: 20-35% of total calories

Micronutrients

Water-Soluble Vitamins: B-complex, Vitamin C

Fat-Soluble Vitamins: A, D, E, K

Major Minerals: Calcium, Magnesium, Potassium, Sodium

Trace Minerals: Iron, Zinc, Iodine, Selenium

Effective Tracking Strategies

Daily Routine:

  • 07:00 - Weigh yourself (after bathroom, fasted)
  • Meals - Record immediately after eating
  • 21:00 - Review daily totals
  • 22:00 - Plan next day

Accuracy Tips:

  1. Use food scale (grams instead of "handfuls")
  2. Measure before cooking (account for water loss)
  3. Check package labels carefully (serving sizes)
  4. Dining out: Search similar items, add 20% buffer
  5. Include cooking oils, sauces, condiments
  6. Track all beverages (coffee, juice, alcohol)

Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • ❌ Ignoring liquid calories (lattes, juices, alcohol)
  • ❌ Skipping snacks in logs
  • ❌ Underestimating portions (average 30% underestimation)
  • ❌ Overestimating exercise calories burned
  • ❌ Perfectionism (80% accuracy sustained > 100% accuracy abandoned)

Data Analysis and Utilization

Weekly Review Checklist:

  • □ Average daily calories
  • □ Macronutrient ratios
  • □ Micronutrient deficiencies
  • □ Weight change trends
  • □ Progress toward goals
  • □ Identify problem meals/snacks

Monthly Assessment:

  • Overall pattern analysis
  • Seasonal changes
  • Stress vs eating correlation
  • Goal adjustment
  • Consider professional consultation
