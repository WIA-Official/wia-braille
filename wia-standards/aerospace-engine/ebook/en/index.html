About This Standard

✈️ The Power that Opens the Skies

Aerospace engines are the core technology enabling humanity to fly and reach space.
From the Wright brothers' piston engine,
Through the jet engine revolution,
To modern ultra-efficient turbofans and powerful rocket engines...
Aerospace engines continue to evolve.

WIA-SPACE-015 (Aerospace Engine Standard) is a comprehensive standard for the design, manufacturing, testing, and certification of aerospace propulsion systems. It systematizes engine technology that powers all aircraft, from civil aviation to military jets and space launch vehicles.

Core Elements of Aerospace Engines

  • Compression System: Axial/centrifugal compressors, pressure ratio, compression efficiency
  • Combustion System: Combustor design, fuel injection, flame stability
  • Turbine System: HP/LP turbines, blade cooling, energy extraction
  • Propulsive Efficiency: Bypass ratio, thrust characteristics, fuel consumption
  • Materials Engineering: Nickel superalloys, ceramic matrix composites, thermal barrier coatings
  • Manufacturing Technology: Precision casting, 3D printing, quality control
  • Performance Testing: Ground tests, flight tests, durability assessment
  • Safety Certification: Type certification, airworthiness, maintenance programs
