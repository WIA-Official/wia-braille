# WIA-PET-LEGACY

## Pet Legacy Management Standard

> "Hong-ik Ingan: Honoring the lives of our companions through thoughtful preservation of their memory and legacy."

---

## Overview

The WIA-PET-LEGACY standard defines protocols for managing pet memories, digital memorials, and legacy planning. This standard ensures that the bond between pets and their families is preserved and honored beyond the pet's lifetime.

## Scope

- **Digital Memorials**: Standards for creating and maintaining digital tributes
- **Memory Preservation**: Photo, video, and data archival standards
- **Legacy Planning**: Estate and care succession protocols
- **Memorial Services**: Standards for tribute and remembrance services
- **Data Transfer**: Protocols for legacy data management
- **Grief Support**: Integration with support resources

## Key Features

### Memorial Types

| Type | Description | Format |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Digital Tribute | Online memorial page | HTML/Multimedia |
| Photo Album | Curated memory collection | Image gallery |
| Timeline | Life story visualization | Interactive |
| Memory Book | Printable keepsake | PDF/Print |
| Video Memorial | Compiled video tribute | MP4/Stream |

### Legacy Data Categories

- Biographical information
- Health and medical history
- Photo and video archives
- Important dates and milestones
- Favorite activities and preferences
- Family and social connections
- Memorial messages and tributes

## Ethical Principles

1. **Dignity**: All memorials maintain respect for the pet
2. **Privacy**: Family controls all shared content
3. **Permanence**: Long-term data preservation guaranteed
4. **Accessibility**: Memorials accessible to authorized family
5. **Sensitivity**: Support resources integrated thoughtfully
