1.1 What is Digital Pathology?

Digital Pathology is a field that converts tissue samples into digital images and analyzes them using computer-based tools.

1.1.1 Traditional Pathology Workflow

1. Tissue Sample Collection → 2. Processing → 3. Embedding & Sectioning
→ 4. Staining → 5. Microscopic Examination

Limitations:
  ❌ Physical slide management difficulties
  ❌ No remote consultation
  ❌ Slide quality degradation over time
  ❌ Limited quantitative analysis

1.1.2 Digital Pathology Workflow

1-4. [Same] Collection → Processing → Sectioning → Staining
5. Digital Scanning (WSI)
6. Image Storage & Management (PACS)
7. Digital Analysis (AI-powered)
8. Diagnosis & Reporting

Advantages:
  ✅ Permanent digital archive
  ✅ Access anywhere, anytime
  ✅ AI-based automatic analysis
  ✅ Quantitative measurements
  ✅ Easy remote consultation

1.2 History of Digital Pathology

Era Milestone Impact
1960s First image analysis Proof of concept
2000s WSI technology emerges True digitalization begins
2017 FDA approves WSI for primary diagnosis Official clinical use
2020s AI revolution Automation & improved accuracy

1.3 Advantages of Digital Pathology

1.3.1 Clinical Benefits

  • Enhanced Diagnostic Accuracy: AI-assisted detection >95%
  • Quantitative Analysis: Objective measurements
  • Multi-expert Review: Easy second opinions
  • Standardization: Consistent diagnostic criteria

1.3.2 Operational Benefits

Cost Savings (Annual, 100,000 slides):
  Traditional: $180,000/year
  Digital: $90,000/year
  Savings: 50%

Space Utilization:
  100,000 slides = ~50㎡ storage
  Digital: No physical storage needed

Quality Control:
  Rework rate: 15% → 3% (80% reduction)

1.4 Challenges

1.4.1 Technical Challenges

  • Large File Sizes: 1-5GB per slide
  • Color Reproducibility: Scanner/monitor variations
  • System Integration: LIS, PACS, EMR compatibility

1.4.2 Current Market Problems

Fragmentation:
  Each vendor → Proprietary format → Vendor lock-in

  Scanner A → Format A → Viewer A only
  Scanner B → Format B → Viewer B only

  Result:
    ❌ Must buy multiple systems
    ❌ Difficult data migration
    ❌ Increased total cost of ownership
