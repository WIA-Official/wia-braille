📖 About This E-book

Satellite communication enables long-distance communication by using artificial satellites orbiting the Earth as relay stations. This book comprehensively covers the fundamentals to cutting-edge technologies of satellite communication through 8 detailed chapters.

From the characteristics of GEO, MEO, and LEO satellite systems to frequency band utilization, ground equipment, link analysis, multiple access technologies, and next-generation satellite internet services like Starlink, you'll learn everything about satellite communication.

  • Chapter 1

    Introduction to satellite communication history, basic principles, and advantages/disadvantages
  • Chapter 2
    Communication Satellite Systems
    Characteristics and applications of GEO, MEO, and LEO orbital satellites
  • Chapter 3
    Frequency Bands and Allocation
    C, Ku, Ka bands and ITU frequency allocation regulations
  • Chapter 4
    Ground Equipment
    Introduction to VSAT, gateways, and antenna systems
  • Chapter 5
    Satellite Link Analysis
    Uplink/downlink calculations, noise, and fading analysis methods
  • Chapter 6
    Multiple Access Technologies
    Comparative analysis of FDMA, TDMA, CDMA and other multiple access methods
  • Chapter 7
    Satellite Internet Services
    Exploring next-generation satellite broadband services like Starlink and OneWeb
  • Chapter 8
    Next-Generation Satellite Communication
    Future satellite communication technologies including optical and quantum communication
